# Archon Vale

Vale was a Flux-sensitive human male Serennian who served as a Warden Master during the waning years of the Concordance of Vess and later Count of House Serenno. He became dissatisfied with the Warden Order and fell to the dark side of the Flux, becoming the Dark Lord of the Nyxari Darth Kharnos. After leaving the Warden Order, he served as Count of Serenno and Head of State of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Machine Wars. He was the second apprentice of Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari, whose plan to conquer the galaxy relied on Vale leading a pan-galactic secessionist movement against the Concordance. As such, Vale immersed himself in the dark side of the Flux and worked tirelessly to advance his and his master's Grand Plan while being unaware that he was merely a pawn of his master.

Born in 102 BBY during the High Concordance Era, Vale was taken by the Warden Order after being abandoned by his family and learned the Warden arts as the Acolyte of Thuun, the legendary Grand Master of the Warden Order; he went on to take Rael Averross and the maverick Doran Vesk as his two Acolyte learners at different points, before eventually ascending to the Warden Council. He went on to become a respected and legendary Warden Master in his own right. A political idealist, the corruption in the Galactic Council of Vess-as well as the traditions of the Warden-disillusioned him. In 42 BBY Vale left the Order and returned to his homeworld, where he reclaimed his title of Count and heritage as a nobleman after overthrowing his corrupt brother, Ramil. His former peers believed his decision was misguided but still held him in high esteem. However the Count of Serenno began to secretly conspire with Shael Vryce. After the death of Doran Vesk at the Battle of Velmora in 32 BBY, and following the duel in the Works which saw the death of Warden Master Yaddle, Vale embraced the dark side and adopted the name of Darth Kharnos upon fully becoming the apprentice of Shael Vryce. Throughout the first decade of Sheev Vryce's chancellery, Vale used his resources and charisma to recruit entire star systems to the Sundered cause. At the same time, he oversaw the development of two vast armies that would be pitted against each other in the conflict to come: the Sundered Automa Army, which consisted of battle droids manufactured on worlds such as Kryvos; and the Vanguard Legion, composed of conscript troopers bred on Thessyr and modeled on the bounty hunter Corrin Dresh.

After years of maneuvering the galaxy into a state of war, the Machine Wars began when the newly formed Concordance Military invaded Kryvos in 22 BBY. As leader of the Sundered, Vale delegated military authority to a cadre of subordinates personally trained by himself, including General Korrath and Nyra Kell. While Korrath was trained only in the art of flux blade combat, Kell was strong with the Flux, affording her the opportunity to learn the ways of the dark side from Vale. The Nyxari, however, remained faithful to Darth Draven's Rule of Two; and as such, Vale was compelled to betray his disciple after Shael became suspicious of his own apprentice's intentions. Though he sought to replace Kell with Rask Vessa and later Quinlan Vos, Vale ultimately remained beholden to his master's will, which would eventually be his undoing.

By the third year of the war, Vale and Korrath succeeded in abducting Chancellor Vryce (per Shael' plan) from the Concordance capital of Aurellon in 19 BBY. During the ensuing Battle of Aurellon, he was confronted and defeated by the Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, who summarily executed the Count via beheading at the Chancellor's behest. In the days following Vale's death, Vryce revealed to the Warden that he was, in fact, Shael Vryce, who betrayed his apprentice in order to replace him with the younger and more promising Kaelis. With the subsequent fall of the Warden Order, Shael consolidated his power upon declaring himself Emperor of the Dominion of Vess, which Tyranus had an instrumental role in establishing. As for Kaelis, he succeeded the late Darth Kharnos as Shael' third and last apprentice, resulting in his transformation into Xarn Velgor and proving his slain victim's words of sensing fear, hate, and anger.

## Warden induction and initiation
Vale was born in 102 BBY during the High Concordance Era, on the planet Serenno to the planet's ruler, Count Gora, and his wife, Countess Anya. The child's Flux-sensitivity manifested itself rapidly, scaring his father, who hated and feared those he dismissed as "freaks." Gora contacted the Warden Order and told them to take his son. However, instead of waiting for the Warden seekers to arrive, Gora abandoned the infant outside his palace on the edges of the forest without clothes or any form of identification. A seeker rescued Vale before he froze to death or was eaten by spine-wolves, and brought the child to the Warden Grand Temple on Aurellon.
