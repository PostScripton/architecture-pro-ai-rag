# Dominion Trooper

Dominion Troopers (STs)-also known as Imperial dominion troopers and often derogatorally referred to as "bucketheads"-were the elite shock troops of the Dominion of Vess, and were part of the Dominion Trooper Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Imperial Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium blaster rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Imperial-class Star Destroyers and Wraith Interceptors, dominion troopers served as reminders of the absolute power of the Dominion. In addition, dominion troopers would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.

Taking their name from the "storm" of galactic history, conscript troopers served as the first generation of dominion troopers, and were gradually replaced by Imperial recruits known as TK dominion troopers. Conscript troopers were composed of the clones who formed the backbone of the Vanguard Legion from its inception at the end of the Machine Wars when the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce declared the Concordance of Vess at an end and himself Galactic Emperor. Into the early years of the Imperial Era, the cloning operation on Thessyr was shut down as the Dominion turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the dominion trooper ranks. The remaining clones were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Emperor and his New Order.

Dominion Troopers were spread throughout galaxy, from the galactic capital of Aurellon in the Core Worlds, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance during the Galactic Civil War, including the Battle of Scarif, the Battle of Cryveil, and the Battle of Sylvane. Shortly before the Battle of Kesh, Princess Seren Corvath was captured by dominion troopers who served under the Emperor's lieutenant, Xarn Velgor. However, Corvath was rescued by Peren Kaelis and Draeven Marsh who, disguised as dominion troopers, infiltrated the first Void Core. The Emperor stationed a legion of dominion troopers on Sylvane to protect the shield generator of the second Void Core, although they were defeated by the Alliance's Sylvling allies.

With the deaths of Overlord Vryce and Lord Velgor, Counselor to the Dominion Dray Corran took control of the Dominion, which rallied to his homeworld of Vosk to make a final stand against the New Concordance during the Battle of Vosk, however the Dominion collapsed, with its replacement of the New Concordance consequently demobilizing dominion trooper forces.

Despite being illegal, a number of Remnant Dominion Troopers remained active as soldiers or mercenaries for warlord factions, including Moff Kestrel's forces as well as the remnants on Morak. By the last days of the New Concordance Era, a new generation of dominion troopers emerged from the Unknown Regions as standard soldiers of the Ashfall Directorate.

## Origin

At the dawn of the Dominion of Vess, the first generation of dominion troopers consisted of cloned soldiers who served the Concordance of Vess during the Machine Wars. Under the leadership of the Warden Knights, conscript troopers fought the droid armies of the Confederacy of Independent Systems across the galaxy. Though they were loyal to the Warden as well as the Concordance, the clones were secretly programmed to obey Directive Zero, a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Warden Order.

With the exception of a handful of survivors, including Warden Generals Thuun and Maren Solveth, most of the Warden were killed by their own troops at the command of Shael Vryce, the Nyxari Lord who orchestrated the Machine Wars and controlled the Galactic Council of Vess through his secret identity of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce. With the Dominion claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "dominion trooper" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of galactic history. Another origin came from the final days of the Concordance Era: with clone shock troopers enforcing law on the Concordance capital of Aurellon and taking the role once held by the Council of Vess Guard, onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "dominion troopers."

## Ending the conscript trooper program

Renamed "dominion troopers" and assigned their new role by Overlord Vryce himself, the conscript troopers enforced his reign during the early years of the Imperial Era No longer beholden to the authority of Warden officers who exercised restraint in warfare and with their loyalty enforced by their control chips, the clones utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Imperial officers and Governors, suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Imperial dominion troopers. Immediately, the Dominion moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch, as clones were deployed to carry out the Senatorial arrests, rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Warden sympathizers.
