# Unit V2-K9

Unit V2-K9, pronounced V2-K9-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (V2-K9), was an R2-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky droid who served a multitude of leaders, Unit V2-K9 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Sundari of Velmora, Unit V2-K9 wound up serving Warden Knight Toren Kaelis during the waning years of the Concordance of Vess, often accompanied by the protocol droid Unit P-EL7 in many adventures throughout the Machine Wars. After Kaelis turned to the dark side of the Flux, the droid served Senator Ryen Corvath for a time in the Imperial Council of Vess. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Concordance of Vess, Unit V2-K9 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Coalition destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Warden Knight Peren Kaelis, the son of his two former masters throughout the Galactic Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Cryveil and Sylvane, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Peren Kaelis until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Warden Order in 28 ABY. Following Peren's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer droid models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Coalition Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Ember Vanguard as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Cinderfall Base, R2 went on to accompany the Flux-sensitive scavenger Nyra Solt on her journey to train under Peren Kaelis on Vel-Tho, the planet of the First Warden Temple, where the droid was reunited with his old master. Unit V2-K9 continued to aid the Ember Vanguard alongside his longtime counterpart Unit P-EL7 throughout the Ashfall Directorate-Ember Vanguard War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Nethyr, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Corvin Dray.

## Serving Ilyra Sundari

Unit V2-K9 was built around prior or during 32 BBY. He was one of several astromech droids on Queen Ilyra Sundari's Velmora Royal Starship during the invasion of Velmora by the Mercantile Guild that year. The ship was used by Queen Sundari and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Mercantile Guild battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only Unit V2-K9 survived - the rest were pulverized by stray blaster fire. Unit V2-K9 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into hyperspace.

As the ship traveled to Kaldris, Unit V2-K9 was taken before Sabé, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Sundari, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Kaldris without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the hyperdrive. Unit V2-K9 accompanied Warden Master Doran Vesk, Bellawen exile Jorlo Wexa, and Ilyra, the queen in disguise, to the city of Dunmar Reach to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Grix they found they could not afford a replacement hyperdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Grix's slaves, a boy named Toren Kaelis, and his mother, Shmi.

At the Kaelis home, Unit V2-K9 met Unit P-EL7, a protocol droid that Toren had built. The two bonded as they worked on Toren's personal podracer. Unit V2-K9 and Unit P-EL7 cheered for Toren during the Boonta Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new hyperdrive. Unit V2-K9 accompanied the group to Aurellon, and from there back to Velmora. As Queen Sundari led an attempt to liberate the planet, Unit V2-K9 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Toren, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Doran Vesk, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Automa Control Ship and shut down the droid armies, winning the battle and liberating Velmora. Unit V2-K9 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed, though they were unable to be joined by Vesk, as he had been killed by the Nyxari Lord Nex Kharn during the battle. Because of this, Kaelis became the Acolyte learner to Maren Solveth, who had been Vesk's apprentice before the Warden Master's death.

Four years later, as Sundari's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Council of Vess, Unit V2-K9 accompanied her retinue to Aurellon on the orders of Velmora's new queen, Réillata. As Sundari's experience as a senator grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the astromech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Council of Vess's break, Sundari returned to Velmora with her retinue and Unit V2-K9, who accompanied her to her family home. There, Unit V2-K9 was called over by Sundari to help her father Ruwee Naberrie build a greenhouse, only for the astromech droid to instead become enamored by Sundari's newborn niece, Ryoo Naberrie. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Sundari told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why Unit V2-K9 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small droid that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. Unit V2-K9 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.
