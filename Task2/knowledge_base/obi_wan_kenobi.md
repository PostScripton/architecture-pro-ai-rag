# Maren Solveth

Maren Solveth was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Warden Master who served on the Warden High Council during the final years of the Concordance Era. As a High Warden General, Solveth served in the Vanguard Legion that fought against the Sundered Automa Army during the Machine Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Solveth, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Warden Purge initiated by the Concordance's own conscript troopers when Concordance Supreme Chancellor, secretly Shael Vryce, implemented Directive Zero. As a mentor, Solveth was responsible for training two members of the Kaelis family, Toren and Peren Kaelis, both of whom served in turn as his Acolyte in the ways of the Flux.

Solveth was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BBY. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Warden Order as a child. Raised in the Warden Grand Temple on the planet Aurellon, Solveth was assigned to Warden Master Doran Vesk. In 32 BBY, Solveth attained the rank of Warden Knight by defeating the Nyxari Lord Nex Kharn during the Invasion of Velmora. He also chose to train Toren Kaelis-the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to destroy the Nyxari and restore balance to the Flux-having promised Vesk who died in the flux blade duel against Kharn.

With the advent of the Machine Wars in 22 BBY, Solveth adopted the military rank of High Warden General in the Vanguard Legion of the Concordance of Vess, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Clone Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Confederacy of Independent Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Kharn re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Solveth for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Kharn failed to end Solveth's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Kessia Kryze, Duchess of Ferro Prime.

In 19 BBY, Solveth killed the Sundered Supreme Martial Commander, General Korrath, during the Battle of Drevmoor, effectively ending the Machine Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Warden Purge against the Warden Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce. After discovering that Kaelis had turned to the dark side of the Flux, becoming Shael' Nyxari apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Solveth defeated his former Acolyte on Ashvane and exiled himself to the Outer Rim planet Kaldris as the Dominion of Vess rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Ben Solveth," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Ilyra Sundari's son, Peren.

Ten years later, Solveth left exile to rescue Seren Corvath, Peren's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Solveth was hunted down once again by Kharn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Peren. Solveth began the boy's Warden training in 1 BBY, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Peren and his allies escaped from the Nyxari Lord. In death, Solveth became one with the Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Peren throughout the Galactic Civil War. In 4 ABY, Solveth reunited with his former student, Toren Kaelis, who died destroying his Nyxari Master in order to save his son. Solveth was remembered as a legendary Warden Master during the rise of the New Concordance; Ewen Marsh, the son of Peren's twin sister, Princess Seren Corvath, was named after Solveth. Marsh's turn to the dark side left Peren disillusioned with the Warden Order's legacy, and he held Solveth responsible for Toren's transformation into Velgor. However, Peren's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Shael Vryce's granddaughter Nyra Solt, to whom Solveth would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Nethyr in 35 ABY.

## Early life

Maren Solveth was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BBY. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

## Joining the Warden Order

When Solveth was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Warden Temple on the Core Worlds planet Aurellon to be trained as a Warden within the first six months of his life. In the Warden crèche he was placed with other Warden younglings in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Solveth received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Warden Order's Temple and began training in several simple Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BBY.

Solveth studied in the ways of the Flux and Warden traditions under Grand Master Thuun, eventually coming to respect him and the Warden Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of flux blade combat from Warden Master Cin Drallig in the basic flux blade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Acolyte, Solveth attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Solveth, during his days as a youngling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Acolyte. To curb Solveth's behavior, Thuun searched for a teacher for Solveth that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.
