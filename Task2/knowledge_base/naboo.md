# Velmora

Velmora was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Mid Rim, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Outer Rim Territories. It was home to the indigenous Bellawen species and to a population of humans known as the Velmora. Velmora was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Nyxari Sheev Vryce, who served as its representative in the Council of Vess of the Concordance of Vess.

Under the guise of his Nyxari title Shael Vryce, Vryce secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Mercantile Guild, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Federation's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Council of Vess for both Vryce and the newly-elected Queen of Velmora, Ilyra Sundari. Sundari led a successful resistance movement and removed the Federation from her world during the Battle of Velmora. Vryce subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concordance, with Sundari eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Velmora would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concordance during the pan-galactic Machine Wars, which saw several military engagements against the Confederacy of Independent Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Vryce had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Dominion of Vess. Vryce's reign would eventually be ended by the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, a rebel movement led by Sundari's children, Seren Corvath and Peren Kaelis.

Despite his origins on the planet, Vryce did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Velmora was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder, a protocol that the Nyxari Lord had put in place to destroy Imperial worlds should they fail to protect their Emperor. Through the intervention of Corvath and Queen Sosha Soruna, however, Velmora was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the New Concordance. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Ashfall Directorate during its war with Corvath's Ember Vanguard.

## Description

Velmora was a small pastoral world in the Mid Rim, located near the border of the Outer Rim Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Core Worlds. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Bellawens, who built their homes in the waters of Velmora, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad. Despite this, certain Bellawen navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Velmora's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Velmora a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Velmora's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Velmora was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Velmora's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak, the planet's oceans, which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer, the sando aqua monster, and the colo claw fish.

## Pre-human colonization

Originally, Velmora was home to the amphibian Bellawens. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Bellawens. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

## Arrival of humanity

Approximately around 4000 BBY, Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu, after one of their principal deities. Velmora quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Mid Rim, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Bellawens retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

While the Bellawens flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BBY. Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Grizmallt crash-landed on Velmora and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Bellawens. Led by Kwilaan, the colonists ventured into the Gallo Mountains, created a farming community at the Dee'ja Peak, establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Velmora calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Kwilaan. When the colonists made contact with the Bellawens, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species. They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.
