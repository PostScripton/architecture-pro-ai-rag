# Zephyra

Zephyra was a gas giant in the star system of the same name in the Anoat sector, its riches manifested in the form of rare tibanna gas. Attracting mining interests and remaining unaffiliated in galactic affairs, Ugnaughts were employed to extract tibanna from repulsorlifted facilities whilst Zephyra's mostly human population resided in the open upper levels, exposed to a thin layer of breathable atmosphere in an area known as a "Life Zone."

## Description

Zephyra was an immense gas giant located in the vicinity of the Anoat sector, a desolate sector of the galaxy. Zephyra had two moons, and its gaseous mass contained a thin stratum of habitable atmosphere above a source of rare and costly tibanna gas, although there was no habitable land or water on the planet. The planet completed a full orbit around its star every 14 standard-years and a full rotation every 12 standard-hours. Viewed from the upper levels of Skyhold, in the breathable layer of the world, sunsets lasted 2 hours.

Tibanna gas was harvested and refined in several mining complexes, including Skyhold, an opulent metropolis suspended in the planet's huge billowing clouds, and Chinook Station, a fuel depot and refueling station. The Ugnaught city of Ugnorgrad also was located over the planet, being ruled by King Ozz in the time after the Battle of Sylvane. Skyhold processed tibanna to be sold off-world.

Zephyra was home to many types of life, which was rare for a gas giant. The planet had two sister worlds, from which it was separated by an asteroid belt called Velser's Ring.

Swimming pools were considered a rare luxury on Zephyra.

## History
During the Machine Wars, General Korrath led Sundered forces and invaded several planets and blockaded several vital hyperspace routes leading to Zephyra, Nyrrath, and Calamarine within the space of a single month. During the Galactic Civil War, Zephyra remained unaffiliated, but that did not prevent the Dominion of Vess from garrisoning the planet and seizing Skyhold. However, with the Dominion's defeat at Sylvane, the gas giant and its prosperous mining colony ended up recovering their freedom.

Following the Battle of Nethyr in 35 ABY, Zephyra was in open rebellion against the Ashfall Directorate.
