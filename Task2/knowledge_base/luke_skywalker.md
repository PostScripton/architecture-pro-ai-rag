# Peren Kaelis

Peren Kaelis, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Warden Master who fought in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Dominion of Vess. Along with his companions, Princess Seren Corvath and General Draeven Marsh, Kaelis served as a revolutionary on the side of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance-an organization committed to the downfall of the Dominion of Vess and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Kaelis became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Warden in galactic history.

The son of Warden Knight Toren Kaelis and Senator Ilyra Sundari, Peren Kaelis was born along with his twin sister, Seren, in 19 BBY. As a result of Sundari's death and Toren's fall to the dark side of the Flux, the Kaelis children were separated and sent into hiding, with Seren adopted by the royal family of Threnos while Peren was raised by his relatives on Kaldris. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Kaelis joined the Coalition Uprising and began learning the ways of the Flux under the guidance of Warden Master Maren Solveth, whose first apprentice was Peren's own father. During the Battle of Kesh, Kaelis saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Warden Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Thuun. After his master's death, Kaelis participated in the Battle of Sylvane in 4 ABY, during which he confronted the Nyxari Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Toren Kaelis. With Peren's help, Toren returned to the light side of the Flux by killing the Emperor and Dark Lord of the Nyxari Shael Vryce at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Foretold One. Following the Battle of Sylvane, Kaelis trained his sister as a first Warden apprentice on Ajan Kloss, but Seren ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Nyxari's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Kaelis became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concordance Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Warden Order, and serving the New Concordance and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Sylvane, Kaelis was heavily involved with the New Concordance's efforts against self-declared Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard in the Anoat sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Vosk. Around 6 ABY, Kaelis committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 ABY, Kaelis rescued Nib, a Ferro Knight Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Thuun, from Moff Kestrel's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossus. During the training, Kaelis was visited by Toren's former padawan, Sera Vantis, where they discussed Nib and how Kaelis sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Warden path. After Nib's caregiver, Kade Ferro, visited the planet bearing a gift for Nib, Kaelis offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Ferro Knight, or take Thuun's flux blade and continue with his Warden training. Nib chose to return to Ferro, while Kaelis bid Tano farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 ABY, Kaelis had taken on Ewen Marsh, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Warden lore, Marsh began to be groomed by Threll, a humanoid being powerful in the Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Order of the Hollow. In 21 ABY, Kaelis started feeling an increasing darkness in the Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Nethyr. Kaelis journeyed to Ilvarn, where he saw a vision of Nethyr and faced nine wraiths when the Flux ghost of Toren Kaelis appeared next to him. Following this, Kaelis was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Nethyr after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.

In 28 ABY, sensing the extent of Threll's corruption in Marsh and glimpsing a dark future, Kaelis momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his flux blade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Marsh's fall to the dark side. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Kaelis that the time had come for the Warden to end. Kaelis therefore sought exile on the distant world of Vel-Tho, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Ashfall Directorate. His solitude was interrupted in 34 ABY, shortly after the fall of the New Concordance, resulting in a chain of events that led Kaelis to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Ashfall Directorate. Before his death, Kaelis declared that the Warden Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Vosk scavenger known as Nyra Solt.
