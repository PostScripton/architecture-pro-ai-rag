# Sylvane

Sylvane, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Sylvane or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Sylvling, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

## Description

A remote moon in the Sylvane system of the Outer Rim Territories, Sylvane was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Sylvling, Dulok, and Yuzzum species. The semi-sentient Gorax also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie. Some of Sylvane's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Sylvane I and Sylvane II.

## History
Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Sylvane to hunt the native Sylvling population to make Sylvling Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Outer Rim.

During the reign of the Dominion of Vess, the Dominion, deeming Sylvane to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Sylvane. In order to make way for the complex, an Sylvling village was razed by Imperial troops, leading to strained Imperial-Sylvling relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Sylvane was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Tarkos Initiative also established Research Station 9 on the moon.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Sylvane that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Concordance and its Sylvling allies against Imperial forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Coalition managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Overlord Vryce and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor, the rebels and Sylvlings held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Coalition fighters set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many hyperspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Sylvane's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Sylvling Civil War brought strife to Sylvane. The New Concordance intervened by sending Unit P-EL7 and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Sylvane. During the New Concordance Era, Sylvane remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 ABY, following the Battle of Nethyr, a Resurgent-class Star Destroyer stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.
