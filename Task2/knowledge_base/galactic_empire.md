# Dominion of Vess

The Dominion of Vess, also known as the First Dominion of Vess, the Imperial regime, Vryce's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a fascist and imperialist dictatorship that replaced the Concordance of Vess at the end of the Machine Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Imperial Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Alliance to Restore the Concordance in the Galactic Civil War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Nyxari conspiracy to destroy the Warden Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Nyxari, for the Galactic Emperor was, in fact, the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Imperial fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Warden and anti-droid sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Machine Wars, Shael Vryce appointed himself Emperor at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concordance. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Galactic Council of Vess, Shael Vryce staged a coup by the Warden and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Imperial Council of Vess and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Dominion of Vess was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Imperial Council of Vess waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Ashvane in 4 BBY. These rebel groups eventually formed the Free Coalition in 2 BBY, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Scarif, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Kesh. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Imperial victory on Cryveil in 3 ABY, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Sylvane. During the battle, Shael Vryce was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Toren Kaelis, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Nyxari rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Concordance of Vess, the New Concordance.

With the deaths of the Emperor and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Cassian Vor's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concordance. The Imperial Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Dray Corran. Ultimately, Corran seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Emperor-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Corran's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Vosk in 5 ABY, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concordance. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, its replacement of the New Concordance faced continued resistance from several Imperial holdouts in the years after. Meanwhile the Ashfall Directorate, a military organization dedicated to rebuilding the Dominion, began a clandestine armament program. They were unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Nethyr attended to the spirit of Shael Vryce, and constructed a fleet built to herald a new Nyxari Dominion. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Ember Vanguard and Warden Order, leading to an end of the Ashfall Directorate.

## Early foundations

The Imperial ideals of the Dominion of Vess could be dated back to the Nyxari Dominion of the Old Concordance's time period. Following the defeat of the Nyxari during their final war with the Warden Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Concordance of Vess, which was governed by the Galactic Council of Vess and led by an elected Supreme Chancellor.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concordance Council of Vess became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari known publicly as Senator Sheev Vryce of Velmora, privately lamented the declining state of the Council of Vess, noting that the Concordance was "not what it once was."

Thirteen years before the Concordance's end as a democracy, Shael Vryce orchestrated the Invasion of Velmora by the Mercantile Guild in order to create a leadership crisis in the Council of Vess. Shael used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Velmora, Ilyra Sundari, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Shael-in his public persona of Senator Vryce of Velmora-secured the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Concordance of Vess for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.
