# Automa

Automas, less commonly known as robots and automatons, were mechanical beings that possessed artificial intelligence. They were used in a variety of roles and environments, often those considered too menial or too dangerous for other species, but also in fields that required extensive specialization and knowledge.

## History

The earliest origins of droids were lost to history, and, by the time of the New Concordance, it was unknown exactly when the first droid was created, though some historians, only taking into account the more advanced worlds, placed the origin of the first droids to approximately 30,000 years before the Battle of Kesh. Nevertheless, for millennia, droids served in galactic society alongside organic beings. According to the B1-series battle droid author R0-GR, the bond between organic and mechanical lifeforms were tenuous with the former fearing that droids would rise up against their masters. Though several droid revolts took place, Roger claimed that an "unspoken understanding" between human and droid cultures kept everyone working on the same team throughout the ages.

Automas existed as far back as the earliest days of the Warden Order, which was founded over 25,000 years before the Machine Wars. One such ancient droid, Professor Huyang, served the Warden for several millennia. As a result of this ancient coexistence with living beings, the galaxy often had an uneven relationship between organics and mechanicals. Records of uprisings and clashes between droids and their organic overlords became increasingly common as droids slowly became more important in galactic society. During the High Concordance Era, the Nihil welcomed droids into their ranks. They also designed blaredroids and scav droids as weapons.

During the pan-galactic conflict known as the Machine Wars, the Confederacy of Independent Systems used a variety of battle droids in its fight against the Concordance of Vess. This mass use of droids in combat cemented the idea of the "droid as an existential threat to biological life" narrative in the minds of the Concordance. Automas were not utilized by the Chiss Ascendancy by the time of the Machine Wars. Ultimately, the Concordance won the war and was replaced by an authoritarian Dominion of Vess. Under the new regime, many people in the galaxy were afraid or otherwise distrustful of droids, due to their bad memories of the conflict.

The Dominion of Vess fostered anti-droid sentiment and treated droids as property. Many sentients continued the Machine Wars policies of wiping droids' memories, fitting them with restraining bolts, and curtailing their movements. Several establishments also excluded droids. Further, anti-droid sentiment adversely affected the labor fields dedicated to droid design and manufacture, which forced those specialized in those fields-such as Maela's mother-to begin working in Imperial droid factories in order to maintain a job. Otherwise, there were simply not enough openings in the market for those skills. After the Machine Wars, the Imperial Council of Vess also passed a law prohibiting programming that would allow for the harming of organics in the droids used by the Dominion. Thus, with both public opinion and the law against it, battle droid production fell sharply. However, this law was bypassed by Arakyd Industries in conjunction with the Imperial Military by marketing their KX-series droids as "security droids".

The illegal sport of droid-fighting was practiced on Nal Hutta and in the Lodge, where Machine Wars-survivor Ralakili ran the fighting pits due to his hate of droids. The Free Coalition, which opposed the Dominion, treated droids not as property but as sentient beings. Several droids were encouraged by the Coalition Uprising and carried out acts of resistance such as bypassing repressive programming, deactivating restraining bolts, and demanding freedom. The Coalition Uprising's acceptance of droids as equals was continued by the New Concordance and Ember Vanguard. Support Automas were units that existed at some stage of galactic history that provided various assistant roles in battle.

## Mechanical "beings"

Automas were mechanical constructs designed to assist organic lifeforms in various tasks. Depending on the nature of the work they performed, they possessed various degrees of artificial intelligence. Most droids had hard-coded restrictions against harming organics. Notable exceptions included security droids and assassin droids. It was the nature of less complex droids to assume that if a human acted as if they belonged somewhere, then that human did belong there.

Automas came in all sizes and shapes, and many of them resembled humanoid organics, possessing an upright posture, two arms, two legs, hands, feet, one thorax, a neck, and a head. The B1 battle droids were designed to resemble their insectoid Kryvosian creators. Likewise, the Colicoids designed the three-legged droidekas in their image. Some droids, like the LOM-series unit, were made to resemble the organics they served.

Contrary to organics, who were born and healed slowly, droids were easily built en masse, were less fragile, and could be repaired easily. They also did not have to learn skills (such as calculation abilities), as they were directly programmed with them. Most droids possessed a form of self-awareness, with advanced models even having emotions such as fear for their existence. Automas did not need to breathe, eat or drink. They were capable of surviving in a vacuum and underwater, and were immune to cosmic radiation, all poisons, toxins and diseases. They did not need to sleep, although they could be deactivated as a form of sleep and had occasional maintenance cycles.
