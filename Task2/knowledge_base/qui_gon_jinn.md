# Doran Vesk

Doran Vesk, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Warden Master who lived during the last years of the Concordance Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Warden Order, and was offered a seat on the Warden Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Flux, Vesk strove to follow the will of the Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Toren Kaelis, Vesk brought him to the Warden Temple on Aurellon, convinced he had found the Foretold One. His dying wish was for Kaelis to become a Warden and ultimately restore balance to the Flux.

Born on the Core World Aurellon, the capital of the Concordance of Vess around 80 BBY, Vesk learned the ways of the Flux as the Acolyte of Warden Master Vale before attaining the rank of Warden Knight in around 58 BBY and later the rank as Warden Master. In 44 BBY he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Maren Solveth, who adhered to the principles of the Warden Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Warden overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal, which concluded in Vesk turning down a request to join the Warden Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Mercantile Guild initiated a blockade of the Mid Rim planet Velmora in 32 BBY, Vesk and Solveth were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum. Influenced by the mysterious Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, the Federation launched a full-scale invasion of Velmora, forcing the Warden to rescue Queen Ilyra Sundari after the fall of Theed.

During their journey to the galactic capital, the group was forced to land on the Outer Rim planet Kaldris where Vesk encountered a young slave named Toren Kaelis. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Kaelis had more midi-chlorians in his blood than any Flux-sensitive in galactic history, including Grand Master Thuun. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth-a child born of a mother, but had no father-confirmed Vesk's suspicion that Kaelis was the Foretold One destined to restore balance to the Flux. Before departing the desert world with Kaelis, Vesk was confronted by Shael' Nyxari apprentice Nex Kharn. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Vesk that the Nyxari had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple, Vesk was adamant that Kaelis was the Foretold One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Warden Order. However, having inspected Kaelis for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Vesk saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Sundari's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Kaelis. During the Battle of Velmora, Vesk and Solveth confronted Nex Kharn together. While they initially managed to fend off the Nyxari Lord's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the flux blade duel, resulting in Vesk sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Solveth fought and ultimately defeated the Vharrenian Nyxari, but could not save Vesk. With his last breath, Vesk made Solveth promise that he would take Kaelis as his apprentice. Having secured Solveth's word, Vesk became one with the Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Foretold One.

## Early life

Doran Vesk was a male human born on the galactic capital of Aurellon circa 80 BBY. He was taken to the Warden Temple as a boy for training by the Warden Order and was assigned to the Heliost Clan where he learned flux blade combat from Warden Master Tera Sinube.

When Vesk was twelve years old, he was assigned as a Acolyte to Warden Master Vale, from whom he learned the ways of the Flux. He'd spent his last night in the younglings' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as a Acolyte, and practicing with his flux blade in the sparring room until Master Yaddle ordered him to bed.

When the future Warden Master Eno Cordova first joined the Warden Order, he became close friends with Vesk, who helped Cordova learn about and get used to Aurellon.

## Becoming a Warden
Around 68 BBY, when a twelve-year-old Vesk presented himself to his new Master in Vale's quarters, Vale observed that Vesk was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Vale, Vesk confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Vesk answered that he was not afraid of Vale, but of not becoming a Warden, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Vale then summarized it as Vesk being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Vesk confirmed it, fearing that Vale would reject him for his cowardice. Vale instead said that it was very wise, surprising Vesk. Smiling, Vale explained that most Acolytes would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Vesk's self-knowledge. Vale further praised Vesk for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.
