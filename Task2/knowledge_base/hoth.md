# Cryveil

Cryveil was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Galactic Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, until the Dominion of Vess located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Cryveil. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Sylvane, rumors emerged about the New Concordance intending to repurpose Cryveil into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Vell Ostara had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Cryveil, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Cryveil looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Cryveil and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Cryveil as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Cryveil was home to fifteen subspecies of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Coalition fighters as a means of transport, and which Draeven Marsh cut open to use as shelter to keep Peren Kaelis and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Cryveil, Kaelis was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Flux, Kaelis managed to get his hands on his flux blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## The Free Coalition

By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Dominion of Vess and the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, Cryveil was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Cryveil as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Kesh, Saponza's Gang, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Cryveil. Cryveil was first surveyed for use as a rebel base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Dodonna, a general in the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, listed Cryveil among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Cryveil a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Veskaran Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Cryveil sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Cryveil provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Cryveil system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 ABY, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Cryveil into a headquarters, Cryveil was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 ABY, after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Vell Ostara gave his list of possible bases to Seren Corvath after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Draeven Marsh expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Peren Kaelis responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Coalition Uprising's new home.

## Battle of Cryveil and aftermath

Cryveil was the arena of a battle between the Dominion of Vess and the Free Coalition, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe droids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Coalition fighters' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Cryveil resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Imperial Blizzard Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Cryveil, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free Coalition during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Sylvane, Cryveil became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Anoat sector.

## New Concordance Era

In 5 ABY, Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Concordance intended to repurpose Cryveil into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concordance Chancellor Vell Ostara had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Cryveil, where the Coalition Uprising had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.

By the New Concordance Era, Cryveil returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. It was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Rask Vantor and DK-RA-43, where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
