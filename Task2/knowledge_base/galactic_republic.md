# Concordance of Vess

The Concordance of Vess, also known as the Grand Concordance or simply as the Concordance, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concordance. The Old Concordance and the first Concordance of Vess were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concordance that existed until its fall to the Nyxari Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BBY, leading to the restoration of the Concordance in that year. Later, the modern Concordance that had been supplanted by the Dominion of Vess in 19 BBY was also referred to as the Old Concordance. Additionally, the High Concordance was a term used to describe the modern Concordance during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BBY to around 100 BBY.

The Concordance was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Kesh, approximately around 25,000 BBY to 20,000 BBY. The Galactic Council of Vess governed the Concordance under the Galactic Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concordance's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concordance, elected from ranks of the Council of Vess to lead the government on Aurellon. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Galactic City, Aurellon was the capital of the Concordance and therefore the seat of the Council of Vess.

The Concordance of Vess and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concordance fought many wars against various opponents such as Ferro Knights, the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, the Nihil, and several Nyxari Dominions. The Warden Order, a religious group of Flux-sensitives dedicated to the light side of the Flux, was sworn to the service of the Council of Vess, and as such the Warden Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concordance. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Concordance, and the last war between the ancient Warden and Nyxari concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Concordance of Vess, ushering in the Concordance Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Concordance's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Concordance-era.

The Concordance entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Nyxari Lords, the Sundered Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concordance loyalists and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Vanguard Legion was organized under the leadership of Warden Generals who led the Concordance Military against the Sundered Automa Army during the Machine Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce-the alter ego of Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari-to control and deceive the Concordance in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Shael initiated the Great Warden Purge through Directive Zero and transformed the Concordance into the Dominion of Vess by declaring himself Galactic Emperor. The Imperial Council of Vess stood as the last remnant of the Old Concordance until it was disbanded by the Emperor at the height of the Imperial Era. In the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War, the Alliance to Restore the Concordance was fully reorganized into the New Concordance, restoring the Concordance of Vess after a generation of Imperial rule.

## Birth of freedom

The original Concordance of Vess was formed between approximately 25,000 BBY and 20,000 BBY and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Threnos and Chandrila. Around 20,019 BBY, the Thisspiasian Blood Monarchy joined the Concordance of Vess in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Concordance existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Concordance," although the government's official name was the Concordance of Vess. The capital of the Old Concordance was located on an ecumenopolis known as Aurellon and would remain there for thousands of years.

At one time, Aurellon's fellow city-world of Troithe rivaled it as the Concordance's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Aurellon ultimately outpaced Troithe in industrial growth. As years passed, Troithe fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Galactic Civil War, a rogue faction of Warden broke away from the Warden Order, believing that practicing the dark side of the Flux would benefit the furthering of Warden power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Nyxari. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Aurellon. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Concordance into uncharted space.

At some point, the Warden Temple was constructed over the Nyxari shrine in order to contain its dark energy. Once it had established itself on Aurellon, the Warden Order closely associated itself with the Old Concordance, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations. Little did the Warden know, the Nyxari had regrouped on the planet Moraband.

Thousands of years before the Imperial Era, the Veskaran royalty sponsored exploration and colonization efforts that helped expand the frontiers of the Old Concordance. Hyperspace travel would prove to be the key to the Concordance's expansion beyond the Core Worlds.
