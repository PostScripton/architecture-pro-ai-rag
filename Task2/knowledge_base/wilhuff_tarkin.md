# Wilhuff Tarkos

Wilhuff Tarkos was an Eriaduan bureaucrat, military officer, and politician whose career spanned the waning years of the Concordance of Vess into the Imperial Era. A member of Eriadu's prominent Tarkos family, the man served the Concordance's Judicial Department before returning to his homeworld to serve as its governor. When the Machine Wars began between the Concordance and its splinter state, the Confederacy of Independent Systems, Tarkos enlisted in the Concordance Navy. He served under Warden General Even Piell until his death on Lola Sayu, where the two were held as prisoners by the Confederacy for having memorized half of the Nexus Route. Saved by a Warden-led rescue team, Tarkos grew to respect Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, who agreed with the captain's belief that the Warden Code conflicted with their wartime duties.

Tarkos staunchly opposed the Warden Order's role as leaders in the Vanguard Legion, believing that peacekeepers should not direct the Concordance war effort. A favorite of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce, Tarkos served on the Strategic Advisory Cell and was promoted to the rank of admiral. Less than a year after his promotion, Tarkos led the military's investigation into the bombing of the Warden Temple. At the conclusion of the investigation, Tarkos was promoted to Adjutant General of the Vanguard Legion, allowing him to act as the prosecutor in the trial of Sera Vantis, accusing her of treason. Tarkos continued to rise in power with the proclamation of a new regime, the Dominion of Vess, ruled by the self-appointed Overlord Vryce.

Joining the ranks of the New Order's regional governors, Tarkos oversaw the needs of the new Imperial Army and, alongside Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart, administered Project War-Mantle to transition the conscript trooper army into an army of conscripted soldiers. As the Dominion's first Grand Moff, he oversaw the Outer Rim Territories and was involved in the secret construction of the DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station. By then, Tarkos was seen as the third member of the unofficial triumvirate that led the Dominion, including the Emperor himself and Xarn Velgor, whom he once knew as Toren Kaelis. As provincial administrator of the regions of space along the galactic frontier, High Warden Tarkos sought to enforce Imperial rule on restive worlds such as Embervale, where rebel cells grew increasingly bold in their opposition to the Dominion.

A proponent of the doctrine of fear, High Warden Tarkos believed that the Void Core battle station and its planet-killing superweapon would terrify the galaxy into total submission. At his command, the Void Core destroyed Threnos, instantly killing billions of Threnosians. He was confident in wiping out the Coalition Uprising by targeting their headquarters on Kesh 4, but he perished when the Void Core was destroyed by Peren Kaelis at the Battle of Kesh. As a result, Tarkos's memory was tarnished in the views of some Imperial officers, such as Grand General Cassio Tagge, who referred to the Void Core as "Tarkos's folly." The Ashfall Directorate, having risen to power as the old Dominion's successor opposing the New Concordance, commemorated the Grand Moff in an effort to portray Imperial leaders in a more heroic light.

## Childhood

[[File:EriaduCreature-AORTarkin1.png|thumb|left|250px|Wilhuff Tarkos was raised on Eriadu, an Outer Rim planet noted for its untamed environment such as the Carrion Plateau. Here, young Wilhuff fights against two Eriadu wild cats.]]
Wilhuff Tarkos was born on the planet Eriadu around 64 BBY into the wealthy but hardened Tarkos family. He lived with his family in the family compound, where the Tarkoss had lived for over a millennium. Wilhuff's father and mother taught him that Eriadu had not always been the safe environment that it seemed-the entire planet had once been an untamed wilderness. The jungle and predators had been more of a threat than pirates and marauders. The early Tarkoss had helped settle Eriadu; they had been their own police force. Later a militia had been formed-under Tarkos leadership. Wilhuff took these history lessons to heart. Wilhuff's parents made sure that he knew that respect, discipline and obedience were of the utmost importance. Because of this Wilhuff thought of himself as a product of a military upbringing. One night while at supper Tarkos's father ordered a servant to take Wilhuff's plate before he could eat-teaching him how easy it is to lose something.

According to a story told by the Espirion Saan, Tarkos had a brother named Gideon, who called Wilhuff "Wil," while Wilhuff called his brother "Gid." The story continued that Tarkos, along with his brother and father, ventured to the planet Eadu in a survey expedition for lommite. In the midst of a storm, Gideon and Wilhuff became separated, with Gideon hurting his ankle in a fall and calling out to his brother for help. Wilhuff, however, abandoned his brother to the wilds of Eadu. Upon reuniting with their father, who revealed the survey had found no lommite, back at their starship, a smiling Wilhuff was asked where his brother was.

In 48 BBY, following family tradition, Wilhuff's grand-uncle Jova Tarkos, along with two other relatives and two Verdani guides, took him on trips to the Carrion Plateau, on which he taught the young survival skills and lessons on the savage wilderness that his ancestors had tamed. After passing a test in which he outwitted a pack of predators at the Carrion Spike, sixteen year-old Tarkos was deemed to have completed his testing on the plateau. Six months after passing this test, Wilhuff's training shifted to space combat. More family members and hired trainers taught him tactics and piloting skills, while admonishing him to apply the lessons he learned as a hunter to control the pirates and lawbreakers of the Outer Rim.
