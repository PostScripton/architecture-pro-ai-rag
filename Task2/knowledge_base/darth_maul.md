# Nex Kharn

Kharn was a Flux-sensitive Vharrenian Kharnite male who was apprenticed to the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, and after his defeat by the Warden Maren Solveth, he established himself as a crime lord during the Machine Wars and the Imperial Era. The renegade Nyxari Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Warden and his former Nyxari Master.

Born in 54 BBY during the time of the Concordance of Vess, Kharn was a son of Mother Vessa, who led the Coven Sisters of Vharren. Taken as a child by Shael Vryce, Nex Kharn was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Nyxari, growing in hatred of the Warden Knights who served the Concordance and the light side of the Flux-while the Warden Order had deemed the Nyxari extinct for a thousand years, Kharn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Mercantile Guild's Invasion of Velmora in 32 BBY. Nex Kharn announced the return of the Nyxari on Kaldris, where he tried to kill the Warden emissaries of the Concordance's Supreme Chancellor. However, Kharn's failure to do so allowed Warden Master Doran Vesk and his pupil, Acolyte Maren Solveth, to bring Queen Sundari of Velmora and a boy strong in the Flux-exceptionally so, to the extent that Vesk believed this "Toren Kaelis" to be the prophesied Foretold One who would bring balance to the galaxy-to Aurellon, the heart of the Concordance.

Nevertheless, Shael Vryce used the resultant crisis in the Council of Vess to elevate his public persona, Senator Vryce of Velmora, to the position of Supreme Chancellor. The Queen and Warden, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Bellawens on Velmora to throw off the yoke of the Mercantile Guild. Nex Kharn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Federation leadership, was redirected to handle the Warden. Kharn dealt a fatal blow to Doran Vesk, but Solveth in turn defeated Kharn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Lotho Minor. Around the same time, Shael took on Vesk's Warden Master, Vale, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Vale set the second part of the Nyxari plan into motion with the start of the Machine Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Vessa plotted against Vale and had one of her sons, Rask Vessa, seek out Kharn. Indeed, the defeated Kharnite was brought back to Vharren and had his sanity and mobility restored by Vessa's magick. Kharn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Nyxari, initially wanting to take revenge against Maren Solveth. After the brothers were defeated by the Warden, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Ferro Prime, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Concordance and the Sundered. Kharn seized control of the self-styled Iron Vigil and used them terrorise Ferro Prime while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Hollow Ring-the Drossmarket Syndicate, Onyx Circle, and the Vexkin Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord-until he caught the attention of Shael, who killed Kharn's brother and captured Kharn.

Kharn was rescued by his Ferro Knight loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Hollow Ring. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Shael Vryce was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Vharren with the use of the Sundered led by Vale and General Korrath. Mother Vessa herself was killed while protecting Kharn, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Ferro Prime, Kharn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Toren Kaelis-the Warden Knight trained by Maren Solveth whom he correctly believed was central to Shael' schemes-alongside Solveth himself to Ferro Prime. Kharn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Concordance forces intervening on the side of the Ferro Prime resistance were instead led by Kaelis's old apprentice, Sera Vantis.

While Kharn and Tano fought at the Siege of Ferro Prime, Shael successfully executed his plan to destroy the Warden Order and anoint himself Galactic Emperor. With the support of some Ferro Knight and Kharnite loyalists, Kharn continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Imperial regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Emperor's Inquisitors, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Kharn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Ashen Syndicate syndicate through Dryden Vos and Reya Voss.

Kharn also sought a great weapon on Malachor purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Warden Kanan Jarrus and Ezra Bridger as well as Sera Vantis. However, they were all stopped by Xarn Velgor, the fallen Kaelis. Kharn escaped and continued to tempt Bridger towards the dark side in service of his own ends. Bridger rejected Kharn's overtures, but not before the former Nyxari Lord used their connection to track his old nemesis Solveth back to Kaldris. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Kharn. Learning that Solveth had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Foretold One who would destroy the Nyxari, Kharn died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.
