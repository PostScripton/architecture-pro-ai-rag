# Thuun

Thuun was a legendary Warden Grand Master who led the Warden Order through the time of the High Concordance, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Nyxari, and during the transformation of the Concordance of Vess into the Dominion of Vess. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Thuun trained generations of Warden, ultimately serving as the Warden Order's Grand Master. He played an integral roles in defending the Concordance during the Machine Wars, survived Directive Zero, lived to pass on the Warden tradition to Peren Kaelis, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BBY, Thuun served the Warden Order for centuries, becoming Warden Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Concordance Era, in 382 BBY, Thuun helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Warden and the anti-Warden Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Warden Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Warden High Council to train Acolytes aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Warden in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BBY. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Thuun reappeared in 230 BBY on Veskara, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Warden battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Warden who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Thuun hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Warden find a way to fight them.

Thuun was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concordance. A leading member of the Warden High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Talon Ebrik and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Velmora, in 32 BBY, the maverick Doran Vesk introduced Toren Kaelis to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Foretold One. The Warden elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Kaelis too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Warden Code. After Vesk's death during the liberation of Velmora, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Thuun's continued opposition to Kaelis's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Warden discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Nyxari, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Thuun was convinced that at least one more Nyxari Lord remained active following Maren Solveth's victory over the Nyxari apprentice Nex Kharn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems-led by Archon Vale, Thuun's one-time Acolyte-threatening to secede from the Concordance by 22 BBY. Unable to defend the entire Concordance against this betrayal on their own, the Warden took command of the newly-formed Vanguard Legion, with Thuun himself leading an army of conscript troopers against the Sundered Automa Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Machine Wars, rescuing the Warden and Senator from execution. For three years Thuun led the Concordance military's war effort as a Warden General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Doran Vesk, who helped Thuun learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Thuun's efforts against the Sundered were undermined by Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari who conspired to destroy the Warden and restore the Nyxari to power. After the Great Warden Purge commenced in 19 BBY, killing thousands of Warden both on Aurellon and across the galaxy, Thuun confronted the self-declared Galactic Emperor but failed to cut short Shael' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Nyxari Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Toren Kaelis-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Thuun's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Murkveil. After the death of Maren Solveth, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Thuun to train Toren's son Peren Kaelis. Thuun initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Solveth's request. After being convinced by Solveth to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Peren Kaelis, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Warden Knight. He trained Kaelis for a time, until despite Thuun's warnings Kaelis hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY, with his health rapidly declining, Thuun died of old age when Kaelis returned to see him. He became one with the Flux, shortly before Peren redeemed his father Toren, who fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One by killing Shael.

Years later, Peren Kaelis's attempt to reform the Warden Order would be foiled when his nephew Ewen Marsh turned to the dark side and destroyed his Warden Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Warden, Kaelis went into exile, just as Thuun had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Nyra Solt, also abandoned her training, Thuun appeared as a ghost to Kaelis. Thuun taught Kaelis to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
