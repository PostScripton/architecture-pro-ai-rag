# Corin Thale

Corin Thale, known as TIE SS-2-5 during his time at Skystrike Academy, was a renowned human male starfighter pilot from Veskara who first served in the Imperial Navy as a Wraith Interceptor pilot until defecting to join the rebellion. He would also serve in the Alliance to Restore the Concordance and years later the New Concordance during their fight against the Dominion of Vess. He became a member of the Free Coalition during the Galactic Civil War and fought in several battles of the war. He fought in the Battle of Kesh, where he flew in Red Squadron alongside Peren Kaelis. After Kaelis destroyed the Void Core, he and Thale were left as the only survivors of Red Squadron.

In the years that followed, Thale became a respected member of Rogue Squadron, flying as Rogue Three during the Battle of Cryveil. Thale and his gunner Wes Janson became the first duo to successfully destroy one of the All Terrain Armored Transports that were attempting to destroy Echo Base, the Coalition outpost on the planet Cryveil, while aboard an T-47 airspeeder. Six months after the evacuation from Cryveil, Commander Thale commanded Red Squadron during the Battle of Sylvane, and he and General Farro Nyle destroyed the second Void Core during the battle. The decisive battle led to the death of the Emperor, Shael Vryce, and left the Dominion shattered in their defeat.

The Free Coalition soon reorganized into the New Concordance, and Thale continued to serve in their war against the now weakened Dominion. Captain Thale undertook a lone reconnaissance mission to the planet Akiva, where he was captured while observing a gathering of Imperial forces that were preparing for a counterattack against the Concordance. Following the rebellion on Akiva, Thale formed Phantom Squadron and took part in the liberation of Groveheim. While Phantom Squadron was disbanded by the New Concordance authorities for launching an unauthorized strike on Groveheim, Corin was able to reform the squadron in time for the Battle of Vosk, which saw the definitive defeat of the Dominion.

Following the signing of the Galactic Concordance, Thale retired to Hosnian Prime to work as a flight instructor. Sometime afterwards Corin married Norra Wexley and settled back on Akiva until they were visited by Norra's son Temmin and his wife Karé Kun during the war between the Ember Vanguard and the Ashfall Directorate. In 35 ABY, Corin fought alongside Coalition veterans Farro Nyle and Grokk over Nethyr against the Nyxari Eternal's fleet, the Last Reckoning, but they arrived too late to save his stepson Temmin.

## Early life

Corin Thale hailed from the planet Veskara, where he grew up wanting to fly. As a child, he watched digi-dramas and holonovels about flying, such as the somber documentary Zero Hour: The Tentraxis Campaign and the memoir Fly Fast and Die Young! He grew up getting his hands dirty at a fuel depot and working local farms in his spare time, and where he became a pilot and a mechanic. While still young, Corin had people taken from him by pirates, and borrowed an old starfighter to chase down their gunboat. He shot a hole in the ship's aft deflector shields and fired through at zero angle, destroying the pirate vessel. He initially felt good, having made his first kill, but afterwards threw up throughout the night.

At a time when Thale was flying cargo ships out of busy ports, the Dominion of Vess recruited him and he accepted as it seemed more exciting than hauling spare parts around the galaxy. Thale enrolled in Skystrike Academy, located on Montross, to train as an elite Wraith Interceptor pilot. During his attendance, he met fellow cadets Derek "Hobbie" Klivian and Rake Gahree. He became aware of the Dominion's cruelty, partly because it hurt people close to him-his family, his friends, and his beloved Mala. In time, his desire to defect to the rebellion attracted the attention of Imperial Security Bureau agent Alexsandr Kallus, secretly a rebel informant known as "Fulcrum," who recruited him.

## Skystrike Academy

In 2 BBY, after Imperial forces massacred the surrendering crew of a rebel transport near Teralov, Thale contacted Fulcrum to defect alongside Gahree and Klivian; Fulcrum in turn relayed his message to the leaders of the Phoenix Cell. They dispatched the former Imperial cadet and Ferro Knight explosives expert Talia Fenn on an undercover mission to rescue the cadets. Sabine infiltrated the Academy as a cadet under the name "Ria Talla."

Thale met "Talla" during a Wraith Interceptor simulator exercise, in which they destroyed several rebel Y-wings but hesitated at shooting an unarmed transport. As a result, their fighters were destroyed by a simulated version of the Ghost, flown by Captain Vult Skerris, who lectured Thale and Wren that the consequence of insubordination was death in combat. Following the exercise, Thale warned Wren that people at the Academy did not usually care about ethics. After Governor Arihnda Pryce and Kallus arrived to investigate rumors of defectors, Gahree argued that they were undertaking risky action, but Thale told him and Klivian to relax.

## Defection

Later, Thale met with Wren at a hangar bay where he confided his unease with the recent simulator exercise. He voiced that he had not signed up to kill indiscriminately, and finally expressed a desire to leave, to which Wren revealed her true name and reassured Thale that the rebels had gotten his message. She then told Thale and his friends to be ready to leave at a moment's notice, but when he asked Wren about her plan, she responded that she would think it up, much to Thale' surprise.
