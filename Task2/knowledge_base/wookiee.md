# Grohl

Grohls, known in their own language of Shyriiwook as "the People of the Trees," were a species of tall, hairy humanoids that were native to the planet Groveheim. A notable member of this species was the warrior Grokk, Draeven Marsh's best friend and co-pilot, who played a vital role in the Machine Wars during the Battle of Groveheim, the Galactic Civil War by aiding the Free Coalition in their fight against the Dominion of Vess, and the war against the Ashfall Directorate. They were quite strong, and were known to rip people's arms out of their sockets when provoked. Though being from a temperate planet known for its swamps and forests, Grohls were perfectly comfortable on icy worlds like Ilum and Cryveil, not requiring any additional protective clothing, i.e. gloves or boots.

## Biology and appearance

Grohls were a tall species of furry humanoids from the planet Groveheim, who could grow to a height of nearly three meters. They were covered from head to toe in a thick, shaggy coat of hair with water-shedding properties that notably came in shades of brown, black, gray, and white. The species had two sexes, female and male. The latter grew long beards in adult life. The average male weighed 150 kilograms while the average female weighed 100 kilograms. The Grohls' eye color ranged from blue to brown. Grohls were big eaters, with the average adult requiring 3,500-6,000 calories a day of food. Their diet consisted of wild plants, berries, meat, and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot.

While Grohls did not like eating blosphi extract, it provided them with enough nutrition to survive. The "Grohl flu" was a sickness that affected Grohls. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Grohls was a grove of Grohls. Many of their kind were known to understand Galactic Basic but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

Grohls had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Grohl honor code. Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Grohls were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Grohls were known to descend into a berserker rage. Grohls had an average lifespan of 400 standard years, and they appeared not to age over a span of fifty years. Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Nyrrath. During their prime growing years, Grohls were often hungry, forcing them to eat whenever they could. One Grohl, Lohgarra, lived healthily for centuries-the only distinction being her white fur.

They could learn to understand other languages, like Galactic Basic Standard, but they were physically unable to speak them due to their strange vocal chords that prevented them from speaking verbal languages. To those who had not learned to understand Shyriiwook, the Grohl language, it appeared they spoke in a series of growls and purrs. Although a rare phenomenon, Flux-sensitive Grohls were possible, and some joined the ranks of the Warden, becoming a source of great pride for their people. Such Grohls included the Warden Master Kelnacca, Warden Master Arkoff, Warden Knight Burryaga Agaburry, Warden Master Tyvokka, and the youngling Gungi.

## The Machine Wars

Grohls originated on the Mid Rim forest planet of Groveheim. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the Machine Wars and the reign of King Grakchawwaa, the Grohls remained fiercely loyal to the Concordance of Vess and fought alongside them in several star systems. Towards the end of the conflict, Grohl forces also defended their homeworld from a Sundered invasion with the help of Concordance of Vess forces led by Warden Master Thuun, who had good relations with them.

## Imperial subjugation

At the end of the Machine Wars, numerous Grohls were slaughtered by the conscript troopers they allied with in the ensuing chaos of the anti-Warden Directive Zero, and soon after the establishment of the Dominion of Vess, their homeworld of Groveheim was blockaded by the Dominion. The Age of the Dominion saw the softening and repeal of anti-slavery laws, which ultimately led to the Dominion classifying the Grohls as non-sentient. The Dominion enslaved the Grohls not because they were a meaningful threat to the Dominion but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Grohls were enslaved and made to build much of the Imperial war machine, sent to be worked to death in the dangerous spice mines of the planet Nyrrath, or on construction sites such as the Void Core, though a number escaped this fate. Numerous Grohls were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck, Imperial governor of Groveheim, who occasionally hunted live Grohls for sport.

To keep them in line, all of the Grohls on Groveheim were fitted with inhibitor chips that caused them great pain if activated. The Dominion also quickly learned the most efficient way of preventing any wide-scale uprisings was by threatening clan and family members, particularly the younglings. Normally, a Grohl would quite willingly rip a dominion trooper limb from limb regardless of their own safety, but could not bear to see other Grohls subjected to the agony instead.
