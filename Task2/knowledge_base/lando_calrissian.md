# Farro Nyle

Landonis Balthazar Nyle, otherwise known as Farro Nyle, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Skyhold and, later, a general in the Free Coalition. Born on Socorro, he was the owner of the Wraith Runner before losing it to Draeven Marsh in a game of sabacc on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Nyle put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Embervale before eventually becoming the leader of Skyhold in the skies of the planet Zephyra.

During the Galactic Civil War, Xarn Velgor arrived on Skyhold to lay a trap for his son, Peren Kaelis. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari forced Nyle into tricking a group of Coalition fighters, including Marsh and Princess Seren Corvath, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Skyhold without an Imperial presence, Nyle felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Nyle alerted his citizens to the Dominion of Vess's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Coalition fighters try to rescue Marsh, who had been frozen in carbonite, from Kaine Dresh, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Nyle joined the Free Coalition and set out to find Marsh. After locating him in the palace of Grutha the Vex on Kaldris, Nyle aided in his rescue. The Coalition fighters returned to the fleet, and Nyle became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Sylvane. During the battle, he piloted the Wraith Runner into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Nyle started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Nyle. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Ashfall Directorate. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Pasaana with Peren Kaelis to find answers about the growing darkness in the Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Nyle settled on Pasaana and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "Hermit" to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Marsh family and was later asked by Corvath's Ember Vanguard for his aid in the war against the Ashfall Directorate.

In 35 ABY, Farro helped the Ember Vanguard and reunited with his old friend, Grokk. Nyle played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Ember Vanguard at the battle at Nethyr, from which the Ember Vanguard emerged victorious.

## Various schemes and journeys

A member of the tragic Nyle family, Landonis Balthazar Nyle, otherwise known as "Farro" Nyle, was born on the planet Socorro. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as sabacc. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Wraith Runner, which he won through a game of sabacc at the gas giant Zephyra. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BBY, Farro won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of sabacc. Farro also developed a partnership with L3-37, a custom self-made piloting droid.

During his time with L3, Farro nearly lost her to a Vexkin after making a bad bet in a game of sabacc on Gonda. At one point, Nyle was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, even showing Brea Tonnika how to work the controls on the Falcon. At another point, Brea and Senni Tonnika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Aurellon in order to get back at Nyle. At some point, Nyle met a droid mechanic who worked on the Mid Rim planet Kijimi, with that mechanic coming to owe Nyle a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Kesh, Farro and L3-37 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Imperial interdictor dropped out from hyperspace. As a result of the ambush, Nyle lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Farro also had dealings with Ashen Syndicate on Verdance. He completed his debt to Ashen Syndicate and its leader, Dryden Vos. At another point, Nyle visited Pantora and successfully convinced Imperial Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.

## Delivering an artifact

Nyle and L3-37 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Hynestia. Farro and L3 was asked by Queen Forsythia Jin of Hynestia to deliver an artifact to the Dominion of Vess for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Rinetta Gan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Zel Gris. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Livno III. Without it, Livno III would fall to ruin and destruction. Rinetta tried to force Nyle and L3 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Wraith Runner as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Nyle traveled to Neral's moon to settle a debt with Ne'eda Frip. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Forsythia again. Queen Forsythia put Nyle and L3 in jail. Rinetta freed Nyle and L3 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Livno III. They all escaped Hynestia and had to defeat Ne'eda's starships before making the jump to Livno III. After returning the artifact to Livno III, they put the princess Rinetta back to Hynestia. Queen Forsythia pardoned Nyle and L3 afterwards.
