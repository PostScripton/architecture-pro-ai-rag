# Grokk

Grokk, known affectionately to his friends as Grokk, was a Grohl male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Machine Wars, the Galactic Civil War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Ashfall Directorate-Ember Vanguard War. He hailed from the planet Groveheim where he was born during the High Concordance Era and became a Grohl military leader. During the Machine Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Warden Commander Sera Vantis, to escape. He later commanded Grohl forces of the Kachirho Grohl Militia during the Battle of Groveheim alongside the Vanguard Legion, led by Warden General Thuun. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Thuun's conscript troopers received Directive Zero from the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce and, with the help of Grokk and his fellow Grohl Chieftain and General Tarfful, Thuun escaped Groveheim and the destruction of the Warden Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Dominion of Vess, Grokk was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Draeven Marsh, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Wraith Runner during a raid on Nyrrath for Ashen Syndicate with Tobias Beckett and Reya Voss. Winning the Falcon from Landonis Balthazar Nyle, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grutha the Vex until Marsh was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Grokk and Marsh were hired by Warden Master Maren Solveth to ferry him, Peren Kaelis, Unit P-EL7, and Unit V2-K9 to Threnos on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance. After finding Threnos was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Seren Corvath escape from captivity and return to the Coalition base on Kesh 4. Though Grokk and Marsh initially chose to leave Kesh 4 and not join the Coalition Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Kesh and were instrumental in Kaelis's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Coalition Uprising.

Throughout the war, Grokk and Marsh fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Corvath and Unit P-EL7, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Cryveil after the Dominion tracked the Free Coalition there. The group sought refuge with Nyle on Skyhold, but Nyle betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Emperor's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Marsh was frozen in carbonite and Nyle helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Marsh from being taken by Kaine Dresh to Grutha the Vex. Grokk and Nyle set off to find Marsh and located him in Grutha's Palace on Kaldris, where Grokk and his friends saved Marsh and destroyed Grutha. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Sylvane, where Grokk helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Shael was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Sylvane and the rise of the New Concordance, Grokk and Marsh fought to liberate Groveheim from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Grokk returned home to Groveheim with his family. After Marsh's son Ben, turned to the dark side and destroyed Kaelis's new generation of Warden, Grokk reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Wraith Runner and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Nyra Solt, Torv Adan, and the droid BB-8. The droid had part of a map that led to the exiled Peren Kaelis, so the group brought the map to the Ember Vanguard that opposed the Ashfall Directorate, which had risen from the ashes of the Dominion of Vess. Grokk soon fought in the assault to destroy Cinderfall Base, a devastating Ashfall Directorate superweapon, a conflict in which Marsh lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Grokk joined Nyra Solt in traveling to the planet Vel-Tho on the Falcon, where they found Peren Kaelis. After Marsh's death, Grokk often served as the sole pilot of the Falcon, including piloting the ship to aid the Ember Vanguard during the Battle of Sablecrest.

Roughly a year after the battle of Sablecrest, Grokk was one of the last of the Ember Vanguard. Having learned of the mysterious return of Shael Vryce, Grokk and the crew of the Falcon, now including Corvin Dray, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Emperor was hiding, being reunited with his friend Nyle in the process. The quest nearly ended in Grokk's apparent death, as Nyra Solt, having been revealed to be the Emperor's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Kest Ren and destroyed a Ashfall Directorate transport that she had thought Grokk had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Grokk survived and was rescued by the Ember Vanguard as they continued the quest. Nyra Solt eventually found Nethyr during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Nyxari, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Nyra Solt finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Ewen Marsh in the climax of the battle, while the Ember Vanguard, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Nyxari Eternal's fleet, the Last Reckoning, that Shael planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Nyxari once and for all, the Ember Vanguard reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Seren, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.
