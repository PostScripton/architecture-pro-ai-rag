# Nyxari

The Nyxari, also referred to as the Nyxari Order, was an ancient religious order of Flux-wielders devoted to the dark side of the Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Nyxari were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Shael Vryce and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Nyxari Lords. Though the Nyxari ultimately went extinct with Shael's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt. 

Thousands of years before the Machine Wars, the Warden Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Warden and his followers turned away from the light side of the Flux, believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Rim Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Nyxari Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Flux as the Warden Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Concordance. Although the Nyxari were driven off of the Core World Aurellon by 5000 BBY, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Concordance's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Warden, the Nyxari were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Darth Draven survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Darth Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Nyxari was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Nyxari Lords-a Nyxari Master and a Nyxari apprentice-could exist at any given time. As a result, the Nyxari were all but forgotten while the Warden focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Concordance of Vess.

The Nyxari remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Shael Vryce and Nex Kharn in 32 BBY. Shael, trained by Darth Plagueis, hid in plain sight as Senator Sheev Vryce of Velmora. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Mercantile Guild, Shael maneuvered the Galactic Council of Vess into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concordance. Over the next decade Shael sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Warden Master Archon Vale, who adopted the name of Darth Kharnos and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Sundered Crisis culminated in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the creation of the Sundered Automa Army which, in turn, compelled the Council of Vess to support the establishment of the Vanguard Legion. Jorlo Wexa, acting in place of Senator Sundari, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Machine Wars began with the First Battle of Kryvos in 22 BBY, after which entire armies of battle droids and conscript troopers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concordance. In the final months of the Machine Wars, Shael sacrificed and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Toren Kaelis, the Warden Order's prophesied Foretold One, to the dark side. Playing on Kaelis's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Ilyra Sundari, Vryce revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to immortality. Members of the Warden High Council, having learned from Kaelis's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Nyxari Lord, attempted to arrest Vryce, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Warden and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Kaelis ultimately chose the Nyxari by betraying Talon Ebrik, allowing Shael to kill the Warden Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Warden Order for treason, Shael declared Directive Zero was in effect, marking all Warden as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The conscript troopers' programming forcibly turned them against their Warden Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Warden Purge, Shael announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concordance into the First Dominion of Vess in 19 BBY. With the former Toren Kaelis, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor's rise to power was complete, and the Nyxari were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.
