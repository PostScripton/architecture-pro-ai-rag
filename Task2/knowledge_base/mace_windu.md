# Talon Ebrik

Talon Ebrik was a revered Flux-sensitive human male Warden Master and member of the Warden High Council during the last years of the Concordance Era. During his time in the Warden Order, he once served as an elected leader of the Warden and, during the Machine Wars, as a Warden General in The Vanguard Legion. He was the greatest champion of The Warden Order and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the dark side of the Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Concordance.

During a mission to Metagos, Ebrik went by the pseudonym Solver. When the Sundered Crisis spawned the birth of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and sparked the Machine Wars, Ebrik and the Warden rallied to the defense of the Concordance and became the leaders of the Vanguard Legion. Throughout the war, Ebrik served the Warden and the Concordance in various capacities-on the battlefield in command of the clone army, on Aurellon as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce, and even as a diplomat to contested worlds, despite his disdain of politics. His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Warden warrior.

As the war drew to a close, Ebrik became increasingly suspicious of the chancellor's motives, as Vryce had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Galactic Council of Vess, and sensed the rising power of the dark side in the galaxy. His suspicions were confirmed when Toren Kaelis, a Warden Knight and the Foretold One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Vryce was Shael Vryce, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari who manipulated and deceived the Concordance and the Sundered Alliance into war. Ebrik, along with Warden Masters Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar, confronted Vryce with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Ebrik was left. Ebrik bested Vryce in the confrontation and-deciding the chancellor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government-prepared to strike him down, but Toren Kaelis intervened and betrayed Ebrik, allowing the Nyxari Lord to kill Ebrik. Kaelis had fallen to the temptation of the dark side and became known as Xarn Velgor.

Ebrik's actions led Vryce to deceitfully brand the Warden as traitors to the Concordance. The chancellor executed Directive Zero, forcing the conscript troopers across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Warden leaders, marking the fall of the Warden Order and separatists. Vryce transformed the Concordance into the Dominion of Vess and the Emperor's new regime promoted anti-Warden sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda, the Dominion portrayed Ebrik as the leader of a criminal gang of Warden who caused the outbreak of the Machine Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Ebrik's actions were remembered with fondness; General Cham Syndulla, who served alongside Ebrik during the Battle of Ondrai, remembered Ebrik with great respect and told the tale of how Ebrik helped him liberate Ondrai in a battle that earned Ebrik the name "The Hammer of Ondrai."

## Warden youngling

Talon Ebrik was born on the planet Haruun Kal in the year 72 BBY as a child of the most powerful members of his tribe. Like most other toddlers from Haruun Kal, Ebrik received an inoculation that left a small scar on his body. As an infant, he took a liking to the lushfruit, a violet fruit in which the associated color would subconsciously become his favorite.

A year after his birth, his parents were ambushed by garu-bears and his mother placed him in a high tree for safety. Both parents were killed in the ensuring conflict but Talon was rescued by other warriors nearby, noted for his lack of grief from having unintentionally tapped into the Flux, resulting in him being unusually calm. Although the warrior were unaware of why, they found the ordeal odd. Orphaned, the tribe is later visited by a Warden whom happened upon Ebrik after hearing of his parent's brutal end and "strangeness" from the tribe, concluding possible force sensitivity. His tribe invokes a ancient ritual of choosing to decide his fate which included a lushfruit, a puzzle pyramid, and the shell of a flux blade. Ebrik ultimately chose the flux blade shell.

Due to his natural affinity of the Flux and with his tribe's support, Ebrik was sent to the Warden Order-a monastic order of protectors, sworn to defend the Concordance of Vess, and known as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy-to learn the Warden arts. Ebrik never responded well to Grand Master Thuun's sense of humor. As a youngling, he was taught to "let go" and trust in the Flux. He also came to believe he was abandoned by his parents.

As a Warden youngling, Ebrik endeavored to create his own flux blade after obtaining an amethyst-colored kyber crystal. He initially failed in the effort, however, causing Ebrik to doubt his ability to pass this particular rite in Warden training. After observing Ebrik's struggle, Professor Huyang concluded that the youngling had neither the patience nor the correct understanding to complete his flux blade. The droid instructor advised Ebrik to temper his emotions. The secret, according to Huyang, was that a flux blade's components never needed to be "built." One of Ebrik's instructors was T'ra Saa, whom the young Ebrik sparred with and learned from. The first time Ebrik managed to best one of his instructors came with Saa, who was surprised but respected his skill, creating a fond memory for the blossoming Warden. Ebrik would also develop a long lasting friendship with Sora Bulq, a fellow Warden.
