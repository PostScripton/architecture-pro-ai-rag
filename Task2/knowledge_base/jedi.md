# Warden

A Warden, often called a Warden Knight, was a devotee to the ways of the Warden Order, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Flux. Adhering to a doctrine that favored the light side of the Flux, the Warden aspired to attain a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation while avoiding emotions affiliated with the dark side of the Flux, such as anger and hatred. Nevertheless, Warden philosophy did not forbid a Warden from acting in self-defense or in the defense of others, and they were colloquially known as Space Wizards to citizens of the galaxy. To that end, a Warden's only possession was their flux blade, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.

Before the time of the Dominion of Vess, Warden worked with the Concordance of Vess with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Warden and the Concordance's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of dark side wielders known as the Nyxari, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Nyxari, the Warden carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Machine Wars twenty-two years before the Battle of Kesh. Faced with a pan-galactic civil war between the Concordance and Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Warden adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Vanguard Legion. After three years of conflict, the Warden discovered that the Nyxari had deceived them and engineered the war to restore them back to power. However, just before the Nyxari were defeated, the Warden were betrayed by one of their own-the Foretold One Toren Kaelis, who had been seduced to the dark side and anointed Xarn Velgor by the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce. Using the Warden's own conscript troopers to initiate a systematic purge of the Warden Order, the Lords of the Nyxari all but eradicated the Warden in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Warden Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile across the galaxy.

The Nyxari ruled the galaxy through the Dominion for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Warden Order. As a result, Ezra Bridger and Peren Kaelis were, in turn, trained as new Warden by Warden Knight Kanan Jarrus and Warden Master Maren Solveth, respectively. Kaelis, the son of Velgor, was also taught the ways of the Warden by Grand Master Thuun and fought for the Alliance to Restore the Concordance during the Galactic Civil War. On his death bed, Thuun gave his final apprentice the mandate of passing on his lessons to a new generation of Warden. Shortly afterward, Kaelis succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Nyxari Master, Emperor Shael, thereby destroying the Nyxari and fulfilling the prophecy of the Foretold One.

During the era of the New Concordance, Kaelis worked to rebuild the Warden Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Ewen Marsh, but Marsh fell to the dark side and became Kest Ren, apprentice of Supreme Leader Threll, master of the Order of the Hollow, and a warlord of the Ashfall Directorate, while Kaelis's training temple and students were destroyed. As the Ashfall Directorate rose to power, Ren and Threll sought to destroy Kaelis, who, in self-exile, briefly trained Nyra Solt in the ways of the Warden until he sacrificed himself to save the Ember Vanguard, after which time Kaelis's sister, Seren Corvath, continued her training. When a reborn Shael Vryce-Nyra Solt's grandfather-revealed himself to the galaxy, Ewen Marsh, the opposite in a dyad in the Flux with Nyra Solt, was redeemed, and Nyra Solt sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Nyxari Lord, preventing the rebirth of the Nyxari Order. Nyra Solt was saved from death by Marsh, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Warden to live on.

## Philosophy

The Warden Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Flux, using the light side of the Flux to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. Even so, the Warden sought to preserve balance in the Flux; the ancient prophecy of the Foretold One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Flux by destroying the Nyxari. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Warden would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus. A Warden used the Flux for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others. Their chosen weapon the flux blade, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Warden took wielding their gifts and that when a Warden fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the flux blade's wielder chooses. Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own, a Warden was supposed to be selfless and work to save others above themselves. Like all people, Warden were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Warden."

Because of their ability in the Flux, a Warden, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless, and some Warden were capable of appearing after death as Flux spirits. The Warden considered themselves instruments of the Flux, and during the time of the Concordance of Vess, peacekeepers instead of soldiers. Striving to be majestic yet simple, knowledge, the Flux, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Warden strength. Instead of acting for selfish aims, such as securing wealth or power, the Warden focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment, helping others in a just fashion, and finding balance. The Warden believed that-no matter one's physical appearance-everyone was internally made up of light. Individuals who did not understand the Flux took to calling Warden "space wizards."
