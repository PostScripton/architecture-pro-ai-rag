# Machine Wars

The Machine Wars (22-19 BBY) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Concordance of Vess and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, which had seceded from the Concordance. Also referred to singularly as the Clone War, they were named after the conscript troopers used by the Vanguard Legion against the Sundered Automa Army. Due to the high volume of battle droids deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Automa War. Furthermore, the existence of the Confederacy granted the conflict other names, such as the Sundered War, Sundered rebellion, and Sundered uprising. With both clones and droids being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Shael Vryce, under his alter ego Sheev Vryce, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Mercantile Guild's invasion of Velmora to become the Concordance's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Nyxari apprentice Archon Vale, he sparked the Sundered Crisis, causing thousands of Concordance star systems to secede and form a new government as the Confederacy, with Vale as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Kryvos, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major hyperspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Concordance victory when Warden Knight Toren Kaelis and his Acolyte, Sera Vantis, secured an alliance between the Concordance and the Vexkin Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Ondrai, Verdance, Malastare, and Thessyr. During this time, the Sundered developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concordance launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Kryvos, which obliterated Kryvos' battle droid foundries, and a failed Warden-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Automa Army General Korrath at Saleucami. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets-including Ferro Prime and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems-struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Concordance Senator Ilyra Sundari and Sundered Senator Mina Bonteri collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Vale and Korrath' bombing of the Concordance capital on Aurellon. Later, a peace conference on Ferro Prime also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Warden worked with local militaries, including Mon Cala's military and Velmora's Bellawen Vanguard Legion, and covertly trained an insurgent rebel cell to retake Ostrivane from Sundered occupation. Similarly, the Confederacy relied upon factions such as the Umbaran Militia and the Zygerrian Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Vessa and her son, the now rogue Nyxari Lord Kharn, strove to exact their revenge on both the Nyxari and the Warden.

During the war's third and final year, the Concordance drove the Sundered into redoubts within the Outer Rim Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Outer Rim Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Korrath launched a massive strike on Aurellon through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Archon Vale, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Shael as part of his plot to lure Kaelis to the Dark Side. The Confederacy quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Korrath subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Drevmoor, and the Sundered Council being massacred on Ashvane. This, coupled with the issuing of Directive Zero and the deactivation of the droid army, led to the total capitulation of the Confederacy and, with it, an end to the war.

Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved-combatants and bureaucrats alike-the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Shael Vryce, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Nyxari Dominion and to transform the Concordance into the Dominion of Vess with himself as Emperor by completely eradicating the Warden Order. Shael was deceiving both sides. Shael's deception was eventually exposed by Shael himself who revealed himself as the Nyxari Lord and cause of the Machine Wars. Evading arrest by Talon Ebrik and a cadre of Warden, Shael would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, who Shael manipulated to betray the Warden and join the dark side of the Flux and molded into his new apprentice Xarn Velgor. Shael publicly and deceitfully branded the Warden as traitors and triggered Directive Zero, which forced the Concordance's conscript troopers to betray and execute their Warden Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Warden Purge. Vryce then declared an end to the Machine Wars in an extraordinary session of the Council of Vess and proclaimed the transformation of the Concordance into the First Dominion of Vess, and the Galactic Council of Vess into the Imperial Council of Vess.
