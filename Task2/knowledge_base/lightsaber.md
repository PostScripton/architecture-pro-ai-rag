# Flux Blade

The flux blade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Warden, Nyxari, and other Flux-sensitives. Flux Blades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's flux blade. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to the Flux by a Warden, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but flux blades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Flux. Though also used by the Nyxari, the flux blade was synonymous with the Warden, with some in the galaxy believing only Warden could use flux blades.

Flux Blades were generally used for both offense and defense. A flux blade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a flux blade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, another flux blade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-sensitive could deflect blaster bolts with a flux blade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Warden could even employ their flux blades to absorb Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed flux blade, though some used double-bladed flux blades or even multiple flux blades at once. Despite lacking Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Korrath was capable of wielding four flux blades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Flux Blade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, flux blades were used thousands of years before the age of the Concordance of Vess, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Malachor. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype flux blade.

## Concordance Era

During the history of the Warden Order, the flux blade was considered to be the Warden's only true ally in battle. As Master Maren Solveth once declared to his Acolyte Toren Kaelis in the days of the late Concordance, the flux blade was a Warden's life. Warden saw the flux blade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the flux blade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Warden did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Warden knew they would need to be more than their flux blades.

When Acolyte Enya Keen lost her flux blade during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Warden without the weapon. While finding a kyber crystal was still a sacred part of a Warden's duty and Keen felt like losing her flux blade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BBY, Acolyte Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his flux blade beam turning transparent and weak. Flux Blades took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Machine Wars came to pass. Warden, previously peacekeepers, became Warden Generals of the Vanguard Legion, and thus began using their flux blades more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Flux-sensitivity, General Korrath was trained by Archon Vale in flux blade combat and could split his arms in two and wield up to four flux blades at the same time. Korrath targeted Warden individually and stole their flux blades as trophies, and by the conclusion of the war, had roughly a hundred in his macabre collection.

## General perception

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only flux blades that were prominently used were those of the Nyxari Lord and Imperial enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Inquisitors who served him, with the Inquisitors eventually vanishing alongside the Warden they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Inquisitors to-as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin-possibly be more well-known flux blade wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Warden Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their flux blades hidden, lest they draw Imperial attention. The Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own flux blades in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase flux blade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Inquisitors and through his own public emergence over the course of the Imperial Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a flux blade.
