# Kaine Dresh

Kaine Dresh was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Concordance of Vess to the end of the rule of the Dominion of Vess. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered clone of the famed Ferro Knight bounty hunter, Corrin Dresh, who raised the boy as his son. Kaine emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Ferro Knight armor. His personal starship was the Nightwing, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Corrin. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Dresh was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Overlord Vryce. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Duneborn of Kaldris for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Dunmar Reach during the era of the New Concordance.

Kaine was created on the Wild Space planet of Thessyr as a result of an arrangement between the Nyxari Lord Darth Kharnos and Corrin Dresh, a Ferro Knight foundling who served as the template of the Vanguard Legion. Unlike the conscript troopers grown from Corrin's DNA, Corrin regarded Kaine as his son. In accordance with certain Ferro Knight traditions, Kaine was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Corrin, whose chain code documented his participation in the Ferro Knight Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Kaine. During the last days of the Sundered Crisis, Kaine and Corrin retreated to Kryvos where the latter was killed in battle by Warden Master Talon Ebrik. Orphaned by Corrin's death, Kaine sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Ebrik, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Aurellon, Dresh took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Threx, Malvo, and Nyra Kell, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Vek Draven.

In the aftermath of the Machine Wars, Dresh continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grutha the Vex, as well as the Nyxari Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Galactic Civil War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Draeven Marsh to Zephyra on a contract for the Dominion of Vess, hoping to deliver him to Grutha's Palace on Kaldris. During the journey, Dresh stopped on the moon of Duskhaven in order to make sure Marsh would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Ashen Syndicate, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Dresh regained Marsh and delivered him to the impatient Grutha.

Dresh tried to prevent Marsh's rescue by the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Dresh survived his ordeal with the man-eating voidmaw, his armor was sold to Corym Dask by Sandkin scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Duneborn tribe, Dresh eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Ferro Knight warrior Kade Ferro on Ilvarn, who had taken Dresh's armor from Dask. After helping Ferro to rescue the foundling Nib from the clutches of Moff Kestrel, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Kaldris where he killed Voss Marn and claimed the late Grutha's throne as Daimyo, with Vexa Rill by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Dresh intended to rule the territories of Dunmar Reach by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Dural Kess such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Drossmarket Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Dresh overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Vek Draven - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Dunmar Reach's denizens.

## Creation and life on Thessyr

Around 33 BBY, an extensive clone army was secretly commissioned on Thessyr by Warden Master Vael Corrin, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Concordance of Vess. The army, however, was later taken over by the Nyxari in 32 BBY shortly after the Battle of Velmora and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concordance and destroy the Warden Order. Prior to the creation process, Ferro Knight bounty hunter Corrin Dresh was recruited by the Nyxari Lord Darth Kharnos. Corrin was to be the genetic template for the clone army. Corrin agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a clone who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Thessyrian scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a clone, Alpha regarded Corrin as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Kaine, he was the very first of many clones modeled on the Corrin Dresh template, but was only one of two known clones that were pure genetic copies of the original Dresh template, the other being Omega.
