# Free Coalition

The Alliance to Restore the Concordance (2 BBY-4 ABY), commonly known as the Free Coalition, Alliance, and the Coalition Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Dominion of Vess and to restore the Concordance of Vess. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Coalition by Senator Vell Ostara, which brought numerous rebel cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Coalition" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, such as a movement secretly led by fellow Senator Ryen Prestor Corvath and a rebel network formed by revolutionary Luthen Rael, who received funding and aid from Mothma. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated rebel cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Ember Vanguard Group was one of the first cells to join what Mothma began calling "the Alliance" before the formal formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance.

The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Dominion of those who wished the Concordance of Vess restored. Its efforts were by supportive senators in the Imperial Council of Vess such as Mothma, Corvath, and later, his daughter Seren Corvath. Its members were citizens of the Dominion or defectors from the Imperial Military, such as Jan Dodonna, Ryder Azadi, and Corin Thale. Founded and united by Corvath and Sera Vantis, the early rebels were assisted by surviving Warden, such as Kanan Jarrus, and his Acolyte, Ezra Bridger. Machine Wars veterans, such as Captain Rex also assisted in the initial movement.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance waged an insurgency against the Dominion, establishing small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling an entire planet. When the Alliance stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Kesh with the help of Peren Kaelis, the tide of the war began to turn, and the Coalition threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. The Coalition Uprising continued to engage in battles with the Dominion in the Galactic Civil War. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Imperial forces succeeded in defeating the Alliance on Cryveil, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance prevailed against the Dominion and critically damaged it by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Sylvane. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Imperial Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Destroyers, and Peren Kaelis redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Emperor Sheev Vryce, before dying himself in the battle. 

The Alliance was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concordance, restoring the Old Concordance and continued the rebellion against the shattered Dominion, lasting until the Battle of Vosk one year after Sylvane, forcing the Dominion of Vess to end the war and finally overthrowing it by the signing of the Galactic Concordance.

## Ember Vanguard movements aided by the Warden

While the Alliance to Restore the Concordance was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Dominion of Vess and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Concordance of Vess was in its last years of existence. During the Machine Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concordance against the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Warden Order helped create resistance units on Sundered-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, one example being the Ostrivane rebels. However, it was not for many more years that such rebel cells would coalesce into a single Coalition Uprising with a centralized leadership.

## Delegation of 2,000

Also during the Machine Wars, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Galactic Council of Vess, which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Loyalist Committee, including Ryen Prestor Corvath of Threnos, Ilyra Sundari of Velmora, and Vell Ostara of Chandrila, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Vryce's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator Meena Tills of Mon Cala was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Machine Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Chancellor Vryce unilaterally abolished the Concordance in front of the whole Council of Vess and anointed himself the Galactic Emperor, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Dominion of Vess. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Overlord Vryce was in fact the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, who had masterminded the Machine Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Concordance. The Emperor immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Warden Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Machine Wars and Senator Sundari's death at the hands of her husband, Xarn Velgor - the identity adopted by Toren Kaelis when he became Shael's Nyxari apprentice - Senators Corvath and Mothma started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.
