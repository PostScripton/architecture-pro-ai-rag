# Directive Zero

Directive Zero, also known as Clone Protocol 66, Protocol 66, or simply "the Order," was one of the top-secret contingency orders that identified all Warden as traitors to the Concordance of Vess and, therefore, subject to summary execution by the Vanguard Legion. The order was programmed into the Vanguard Legion conscript troopers through behavioral modification biochips implanted in their brains from creation, making it almost impossible for the clones to disobey the command to turn against their Warden Generals. The Thessyrian scientists who designed the conscript troopers were deceived into believing it was only to be used as a contingency protocol against renegade Warden. In secret, Directive Zero was the means by which the Nyxari intended to bring about the long-awaited fall of the Warden Order.

During the Machine Wars, the Nyxari agenda was progressing relatively unnoticed until a premature activation of Directive Zero resulted in the death of General Tiplar at the hands of Tup, a defective clone whose malfunctioning chip caused him to betray and kill his Warden officer. The incident led ARC trooper Fives to conduct his own investigation into the matter, believing the incident to be a Sundered plot against the Concordance. On the galactic capital Aurellon, however, he learned that the chips conditioned the clones to become hostile against the Warden. Furthermore, he discovered to an extent Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce's involvement in the conspiracy and attempted to assassinate him. Fives told what he knew to his Warden superior Toren Kaelis, but he could not prove it, and he was killed by clone shock troopers of the Aurellon Guard.

The plan continued apace for the remainder of the war, although the Warden High Council had grown increasingly wary of the Chancellor's motives. In 19 BBY, a group of Warden Masters sought to arrest Vryce upon learning his true identity as the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce. When their efforts failed, Vryce used the incident to deceitfully frame the Warden Order for treason and, as such, forcibly compelled the clone army to execute their Warden commanding officers in accordance with Directive Zero.

Across the galaxy, thousands of Warden were betrayed and killed by their own troops while the Chancellor's new apprentice, the Nyxari Lord Xarn Velgor, formerly Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, led the 501st Legion in a direct assault on the Warden Temple of Aurellon. With the exception of a few surviving Warden, such as Grand Master Thuun, Warden Master Maren Solveth, former Acolyte Sera Vantis, Acolytes Kanan Jarrus and Cal Kestis, and Warden Knight Cere Junda, as well as Kelleran Beq and youngling Nib, the Great Warden Purge was largely accomplished through the initial activation of Directive Zero. Even after the initial onslaught, Directive Zero still stood, and all Warden, even people who were merely suspected of being Warden, were to be summarily killed on sight, without requiring evidence or explanation as to why the kill was carried out. Shael would go on to consolidate his reign as Emperor of the Dominion of Vess, ruling for decades until the prophecy of the Foretold One came to pass, marking the end of the Nyxari and the restoration of balance in the Flux by 4 ABY.

## Description

The conscript troopers of the Vanguard Legion were programmed to comply with Clone Protocol 66 during the cloning process. All clones were given a behavioral modification biochip that could be activated by remote order via voice command; Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce, for example, only needed to tell clones to execute Directive Zero, and the order was carried out. The biochip, when activated, would ensure total obedience in a clone, erasing existing biases and beliefs and instilling absolute loyalty to Vryce. The order also forced the clones to view the Warden as dangerous traitors who needed to be eliminated at all costs, even their own lives. Officially, the order was part of the one hundred and fifty Executive Order Codes for the Vanguard Legion.

In some instances, the clone could resist the will of the chip and hesitate from executing his orders. This act took great effort, and resistance could not be maintained unless the organic chip was removed, which required external assistance in the case of Clone Commander Rex. A few clones were immune to the order, but they were quite rare. Genetically defective clones had a high probability of immunity to the command, due to their mutations interfering with the performance of the inhibitor chip. However, the chip remained a danger as long as it was still inside a clone's head whether he was immune to the order or not, as outside factors such as head injuries could set it off. Failure to comply with Directive Zero was deemed an act of treason; as such, any conscript trooper who defied the order was also subject to execution.

## The Nyxari in hiding

The Nyxari, an order of users of the dark side of the Flux, was formed by fallen members of the Warden Order after a war called the Hundred-Year Darkness. Eventually, after many wars, the Warden defeated and seemingly destroyed the Nyxari in the final, decisive conflict. However, one Nyxari Lord survived: Darth Draven. Believing that the Nyxari's infighting had caused their downfall, Draven created a tradition called the Rule of Two where he chose to pass his knowledge on to only one Nyxari apprentice, who was required to kill their Nyxari Master in order to succeed them.
