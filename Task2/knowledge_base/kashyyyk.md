# Groveheim

Groveheim, also known as Planet Grohl C to some humans in the Core Worlds, was a wroshyr tree-covered forest planet located in the southwestern quadrant of the galaxy and the homeworld of the Grohl species. Four millennia before the Battle of Kesh, Groveheim was discovered by the Ferrox Combine, who enslaved the Grohl population and renamed the planet G5-623, later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Grohls until an uprising drove the oppressors away. During the Machine Wars, Groveheim was a member of the Concordance of Vess, and endured enslavement under the Dominion of Vess, the Concordance's successor. Later, during the rise of the New Concordance, Groveheim was liberated with the help of Concordance forces led by Draeven Marsh.

## Description

Groveheim was a temperate jungle planet orbiting around a single star located in the Mytaranor sector of the Mid Rim. The planet had a complement of three moons, one of which was orange. The planet had no axial tilt and a perfectly circular orbit, causing it to lack seasons. The center of the planet was covered with a tropical ocean belt, which contained a number of islands and large coral reefs.

Covered in wroshyr trees, Groveheim served as homeworld to the Grohl species. Native fauna, such as Can-cells would influence the design of starships by the Grohl inhabitants, while great cities, such as Kachirho would be built into the planet's trees. The Groveheimian wilds could be dangerous to many, thanks to dangerous animals, such as Terentateks or wyyyschokk spiders in addition to carnivorous plants, such as the jaw plant and saava.

The crash site of the prison ship Ashmead's Lock was known as the Black Forest. Grohls believed that the Black Forest was cursed and haunted by bad spirits. By 5 ABY, sections of Groveheim's forests had been deforested and several rivers had been drained. The Dominion also established undersea mining platforms. Mount Arayakyak was once a rainforest orchard known as the Cultivating Talon, which provided Grohls with fruits, such as shi-shok. By the Imperial Era, it had been defoliated by a poisonous black mold.

Two years after the planet's liberation by the New Concordance, much of the Black Forest had grown back. The growing forest was still littered with the remnants of Imperial machinery, including an AT-ACT walker.

Groveheim was in the same star system as Trandosha, the home of the Trandoshan species, who were traditional enemies of the Grohls.

## The Zeffo and Ferrox occupation
In its early history, the Zeffo, an ancient technologically advanced species, travelled to Groveheim where they had contact with the Grohls. Millennia before the Galactic Civil War, the Predori prison ship Ashmead's Lock crashed on the planet when a nearby gravity well collapsed on itself. 

Four millennia before the Battle of Kesh, Groveheim was discovered by the Ferrox Combine, who renamed the planet G5-623, and later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Grohls until an uprising drove the invaders away. That painful episode left lasting scars in the Grohls' hearts.

## Machine Wars

In the years following the Invasion of Velmora, the rogue Warden Archon Vale fomented political turmoil on the planet. Due to its status as a major navigational point, Groveheim was of prime strategic importance to the Concordance of Vess. After the Grohls fought off its raiders, the Confederacy of Independent Systems attacked the planet toward the end of the Machine Wars. During the battle for their homeworld, hundreds of the Grohl's catamarans were deployed. Eventually, the Confederacy's invasion fleet was driven off by Warden General Master Thuun's taskforce, but a massive force of LM-432 crab droids, droid tanks, and gunships remained and occupied the Wawaatt Archipelago.

In the last days of the Machine Wars, with the help of Thuun and Warden Master Luminara Unduli, the Vanguard Legion managed to get the upper hand over the Sundered. However, shortly after that victory, the Supreme Chancellor of the Concordance Sheev Vryce unilaterally transformed the government into the First Dominion of Vess. While Thuun was able to escape from the conscript troopers that attacked him after the initiation of Directive Zero, Unduli was captured and taken to Stygeon Prime's prison, the Spire.

## Imperial repression

Despite Groveheim's loyalty to the Concordance, the self-appointed Emperor had the Grohls enslaved, and their planet itself was blockaded, when the Dominion launched a siege of the planet. Used as slave labor, the Dominion would utilize the Grohls to cut down the planet's native wroshyr trees for lumber and farm food to feed the Imperial Army as well as send them off to labor camps and construction jobs, such as building the first Void Core, or for science and bio-weapon experiments. In addition to being occupied and enslaved, Groveheim was also stripped of its name and designated Imperial territory G5-623.

Several months into Groveheim's occupation, Trandoshan mercenaries were contracted by the Dominion and supplied with Incinerator tanks. Under the supervision of clone dominion troopers, the Trandoshans were tasked with burning down large sectors of forest on the planet in an effort to make way for Imperial industrialization. Directive Zero survivor Acolyte Gungi later assisted a tribe of Grohls in destroying a convoy of these tanks.
