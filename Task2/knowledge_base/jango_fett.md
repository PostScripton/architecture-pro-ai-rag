# Corrin Dresh

Corrin Dresh was a famed Ferro Knight human male bounty hunter and the clone template of the Vanguard Legion. Known as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy during the final years of the Concordance of Vess, Dresh was proficient in marksmanship as well as unarmed combat. The Ferro Knight armor that he wore featured various weapons and gadgets, including a flamethrower, dual WESTAR-34 blaster pistols, and a jetpack. His personal starship was the Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft Nightwing.

Hailing from the planet Concord Dawn, Dresh was a foundling of the Ferro Knights trained by a mentor known partially as Jaste, and, as a Ferro Knight warrior, fought in the Ferro Knight Civil Wars before becoming a bounty hunter. As a bounty hunter, Dresh worked with and became acquainted with several fellow hunters and individuals in the criminal underworld including the Duros Vek Draven, whom Dresh once mentored, Tessa Korr, Riva Kesh, and the infamous pirate Rask Vantor. In time, Dresh became widely regarded as the galaxy's best bounty hunter, with a notoriety matched by few other bounty hunters.

Shortly after the Invasion of Velmora, the Nyxari Lord Darth Kharnos hired Dresh to serve as the genetic foundation of an army secretly bred on the Wild Space world of Thessyr. His DNA spawned millions of conscript troopers who shared his face, voice, and were trained to become soldiers. As part of his payment, Dresh was given an unaltered clone whom he raised as his son, Kaine Dresh. In the last days of the Sundered Crisis, Dresh was involved in the failed plot to assassinate Senator Ilyra Sundari on Aurellon. The Warden Order investigated the incident, forcing Dresh to leave the Thessyrian homeworld after he was confronted by Warden Knight Maren Solveth.

Solveth tracked Dresh to Kryvos where he discovered the bounty hunter's connection with Archon Vale and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Dresh fought against the Warden assault team during the First Battle of Kryvos, and was beheaded by Warden Master Talon Ebrik in 22 BBY. His legacy survived not only through the millions of conscript troopers that formed the backbone of the Concordance Military, but also Kaine, who became a bounty hunter like his father before him. By the era of the Dominion of Vess, Kaine possessed the same set of Ferro Knight armor worn by Corrin, although its appearance had been altered significantly.

## Early life

Corrin Dresh was born in 66 BBY and was from the Ferro Knight colony planet Concord Dawn, a world within the Ferro Prime sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Although he told others he came from this Ferro Knight world, his exact history remained unknown, much to Dresh's enjoyment. As a boy, Dresh was a prodigy in unarmed combat. Following the murder of his parents, Dresh was found on Concord Dawn by and taken into ranks of the Ferro Knights as a foundling. The legendary warriors raised him to become a Ferro Knight warrior. He was taught by a mentor known partially as Jaste.

Dresh participated in the Ferro Knight Civil Wars, and it was during the wars that he was first given his own set of distinctive Ferro Knight armor. Eventually, however, his time with the Ferro Knights led him to pursue a career as a bounty hunter. Although he would retain his Ferro Knight armor, Prime Minister Almec of the New Ferro Knights disavowed any connection to Dresh, claiming that he was simply a bounty hunter who had somehow stolen an artifact from Ferro Prime's troubled past.

## Rise of Corrin Dresh

During the final years of the Concordance of Vess, but still many years prior to the Invasion of Velmora, Dresh, over time, rose to the top of the bounty hunting trade, where he expertly used his advanced armor and twin blaster pistols to hunt down fugitives. Initially, Dresh was not able to just take on the jobs that he wanted, and it took him many years to build his reputation until he could operate as he chose to and saw fit. Ultimately, Dresh became widely regarded as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy. At some point in his career Dresh began to work with Riva Kesh as a frequent accomplice. Dresh was also an associate of pirate captain Rask Vantor and fellow bounty hunter Tessa Korr. Once being mentored by the legendary man, Vek Draven, a Duros who was a major bounty hunter by the time of the Machine Wars, came to owe Dresh several favors that he never paid off.

At one point in time, Dresh and Wesell journeyed to a terrestrial astronomical object called Snugano. While there, Dresh and Wesell used a tactic, with his companion nearly dying using her abilities as a Clawdite to morph into a fur-covered beast. At another point, Dresh undertook another job to Oovo 4, a prison moon that Firespray attack craft were used to guard. During this event, Dresh stole a Firespray-31 starship, naming it Nightwing, utilizing it as his personal transport, and upgrading the craft extensively, outfitting the vessel with a number of weapons, including laser cannons, projectile launchers, and seismic charges.

Between 40 BBY and 34 BBY, after a thief known as Phaedra robbed the Galactic Bank of Aurellon for 10,000 credits, Dresh was hired by Grutha the Vex to track down her and the money. He followed her to Malastare, where the two briefly fought before striking a deal together. Dresh agreed to help Phaedra elude Marshal Buck Vancto of the Benelex Marshal Service in exchange for three times what Grutha was paying. Two days later, Dresh met up with Phaedra once more on Malastare and was given 10% of her bet at a trifecta.
