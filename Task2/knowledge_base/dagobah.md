# Murkveil

Murkveil was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murkveil system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Warden Grand Master Thuun during his exile after the destruction of the Warden Order. Peren Kaelis received advanced training in the ways of the Flux under Warden Master Thuun on Murkveil, and it was later the place of Thuun's death and transformation into the Flux.

## Description

Murkveil was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures-such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs-living on world. Warden Master Thuun would also make a home for himself on Murkveil during his exile. Murkveil was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Murkveil were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Vel-Tho. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the dark side of the Flux on Murkveil. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murkveil until Thuun's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the light side of the Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Imperial Era, Murkveil's ecosystem saw balance until Thuun's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murkveil once more.

Murkveil had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## Concordance Era

During the High Concordance Era, an academic paper was written that described Murkveil as a world strong in the Flux.

During the Concordance Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Murkveil in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Concordance of Vess and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Machine Wars, Warden Grand Master Thuun traveled to the planet Murkveil after hearing the voice of deceased Warden Master Doran Vesk. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Vesk, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Thuun of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Thuun entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Warden fighting the clones and then the mysterious Nyxari Lord.

## Thuun's exile

After his failure to defeat the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce following the formation of the Dominion of Vess, Thuun exiled himself to Murkveil. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Flux. The lifeboat served as Thuun's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Murkveil's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Thuun built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Murkveil wet season created a need for the Warden Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Murkveil in search of Thuun. After saving her from a monster, Thuun used the Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Thuun watched from afar.

## Training ground of Peren Kaelis

Years later, Peren Kaelis of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance traveled to Murkveil under the direction of late Warden Master Maren Solveth in hopes of finding Thuun and being trained in the ways of the Warden Order. Arriving with his astromech Unit V2-K9, Kaelis crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Warden, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Thuun at the time. The old master took Kaelis back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Kaelis. Initially reluctant to train Kaelis due to his age and lack of patience, Thuun was eventually convinced to teach him by the Flux spirit of Solveth.

One night during Kaelis's training, an Imperial probe droid from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The droid's operator, Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Kaelis's Talon-fighter in the swamp. Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Talon-fighter, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Cryveil, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next droid.
