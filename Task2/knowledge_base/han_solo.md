# Draeven Marsh

Draeven Marsh, known only as Draeven until being given the surname Marsh, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Wraith Runner, a leader in the Free Coalition and an instrumental figure in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Dominion of Vess. Although he did not believe in the Flux controlling his destiny, Draeven came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Warden Peren Kaelis and Princess Seren Corvath in the fight for the Coalition Uprising. Following the war, Draeven and Seren welcomed a son, Ewen Marsh, who became the dark knight Kest Ren after being corrupted by the dark side of the Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Seren hoped that Draeven, as Ben's father, could find a way to break the dark side's hold on their son. Draeven Marsh became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Coalition Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Veskara, Draeven yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Reya Voss, Draeven lived as a street thief with Veskara's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Draeven to flee Veskara. He was given the full name "Draeven Marsh" by an Imperial recruitment officer while entering the Imperial Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Imperial cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Imperial Armored Division. On Mimban, Draeven met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Nyrrath in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Ashen Syndicate crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Reya Voss, Draeven was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Draeven also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Farro Nyle and the Grohl Grokk, the latter who became his best friend. Nyle owned the Wraith Runner, a fast ship with which Draeven made the Nyrrath Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Farro in a game of sabacc.

With only Grokk remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Veskaran smuggler, Draeven Marsh embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grutha the Vex, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Kaldris. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Marsh owed a great deal of money to Grutha, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Draeven and Grokk were hired to charter Peren Kaelis, Maren Solveth, and the droids Unit V2-K9 and Unit P-EL7 to Threnos, avoiding Imperial dominion troopers who were searching for Peren's droids. However, as they find Threnos destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Marsh became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Seren Corvath, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Coalition base on Kesh 4, and saved Peren from the Nyxari Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Kesh, allowing Kaelis to destroy the Void Core. Marsh fought with the Coalition Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Cryveil, Marsh sought refuge with Corvath-with whom he had fallen in love-Grokk, and Unit P-EL7 on Skyhold, which was operated by his old friend Farro Nyle. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Kaelis to the city. Marsh was frozen in carbonite and taken to Grutha's Palace on Kaldris by the bounty hunter Kaine Dresh, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Marsh was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Sylvane. Marsh led the Pathfinders to Sylvane, where he, Corvath, Grokk, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Sylvlings, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Sylvane, which saw the deaths of Emperor Sheev Vryce and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Coalition Uprising to form the New Concordance. In the early days after Sylvane, Marsh continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Corvath, and the two had a son, Ewen Marsh. The boy struggled with the dark side of the Flux-like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Peren Kaelis, Ben was Flux-sensitive-so Corvath sent him to be trained as a Warden by Kaelis. Ben was seduced to the dark side by Threll, Supreme Leader of the Ashfall Directorate, resulting in the destruction of Kaelis's attempt to rebuild the Warden Order.
