# Vryce

Shael Vryce, born Sheev Vryce and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Nyxari and Emperor of the Dominion of Vess, with his reign lasting from 19 BBY to 4 ABY. Rising to power in the Galactic Council of Vess as the senator of Velmora, the secretive Nyxari Lord cultivated two identities, Shael and Vryce, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Concordance of Vess and the Warden Order through the Machine Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Sylvane. However, the dark side of the Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Nethyr by Nyra Solt, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast clone son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Mid Rim planet Velmora in 84 BBY as the High Concordance Era ended, Shael possessed a strong connection to the Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Nyxari by Darth Plagueis. He eventually betrayed and killed his Nyxari Master, in order to train his own Nyxari apprentice, Nex Kharn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Nyxari Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Velmora, a crisis orchestrated by Shael, led to the Council of Vess to elect Vryce as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Kharn, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Velmora. The Sundered Crisis and the Machine Wars were masterminded by Shael and his second apprentice, Darth Kharnos, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Warden Purge commenced with Shael enacting Directive Zero.

With the Warden all but extinct, Shael installed the New Order in the Concordance's place, declaring himself Galactic Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Tyranus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Warden Knight Toren Kaelis to the dark side and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concordance. During the Galactic Civil War, the Emperor sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Peren Kaelis. However, Shael' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the dark side and killed Shael to protect Peren. Thus, with the Nyxari's destruction, the Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Toren's destiny as the prophesied Foretold One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Shael transferred his essence to an imperfect clone vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Dominion of Vess and replace it with a new Nyxari Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concordance and the emergence of the Ashfall Directorate, the creation of Supreme Leader Threll, who turned Ewen Marsh-heir to the Kaelis bloodline-to the dark side, and the establishment of the Last Reckoning. When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 ABY, the Ember Vanguard invaded the planet Nethyr, Shael' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in the Flux in the form of the bond between Marsh and Nyra Solt, a Acolyte sired from the Vryce bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nyra Solt called upon the strength of past Warden to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Shael and his Nyxari Eternal cultists.

## Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Shael Vryce were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Rim planet Velmora as Sheev Vryce, eighty-four years before the Battle of Kesh. The dynastic, noble Vryce family belonged to the affluent class in Velmora society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Vryce did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Vryce was raised in the cultured city of Theed, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Vryce had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Shael came to study under the Nyxari were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Nyxari apprentice to Dark Lord of the Nyxari Darth Plagueis and took on the name Shael Vryce by 52 BBY. As a Nyxari Lord, Shael learned all his Nyxari Master knew about the dark side of the Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Darth Draven. While apprenticed to Plagueis, Shael constructed his own Nyxari flux blades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.
