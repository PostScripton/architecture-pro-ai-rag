# Ilyra Sundari

Ilyra Sundari Naberrie, also simply known as Ilyra Sundari, was a human female politician who represented the people of Velmora during the final years of the Concordance of Vess. Prior to her career as a senator in the Galactic Council of Vess, Sundari was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Velmora. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Machine Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Warden Knight Toren Kaelis would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BBY during the Age of the Concordance, Ilyra Naberrie was raised by her parents, Ruwee Naberrie and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Mid Rim world of Velmora. Despite the humble background of the Naberrie family, Ilyra was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Sundari" in 32 BBY. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Sheev Vryce, Sundari's reign was put to the test by the Mercantile Guild's blockade and subsequent invasion of Velmora. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle droids without assistance, the queen beseeched the Galactic Council of Vess on Aurellon for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Warden Order and the Bellawen Vanguard Legion, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Valorum enabled Vryce to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Sundari joined the Council of Vess and struggled to resolve the Sundered Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BBY. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Mercantile Guild, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Velmora under the protection of Acolyte Toren Kaelis. Though the Warden Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Warden, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Kryvos, Sundari secretly married her Warden protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Machine Wars, with Sundari continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Council of Vess while Kaelis served on the front lines as a Warden General in the Vanguard Legion.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Sundari revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Kaelis's fear of loss, he was seduced by the dark side of the Flux, believing that by becoming a Nyxari Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Sundari witnessed Overlord Vryce's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concordance had become the Dominion of Vess. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Nyxari, she confronted him on Ashvane in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Peren Kaelis and Seren Sundari Kaelis. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Toren Kaelis, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Sundari's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Seren entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Alliance to Restore the Concordance and its successor, the New Concordance, before becoming the founding general of the Ember Vanguard against the Ashfall Directorate-a successor state to the Dominion of Vess. Peren, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Warden like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Coalition. Through Peren's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Sundari's last words proved true when Toren killed the Emperor during the Battle of Sylvane.

## Early life
A human female of the Naberrie family, the young woman best remembered as Ilyra Sundari was born Ilyra Naberrie on the bountiful planet of Velmora in 46 BBY. She was the younger daughter of Ruwee and Jobal Naberrie and the sister of Sola Naberrie. Her father was a friend of Senator Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Rodia in the Council of Vess of the Concordance of Vess. Naberrie was very fond of the Verdani, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ono."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Naberrie was seven, she traveled with her father and Farr to Shadda-Bi-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Shadda-Bi-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

## Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Naberrie was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Theed Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Naberrie participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Palo Jemabie. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Jemabie left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two-year mandate as supervisor of Theed, the capital city of Velmora.
