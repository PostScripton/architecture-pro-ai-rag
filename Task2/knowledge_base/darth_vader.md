# Xarn Velgor

Toren Kaelis was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Warden Knight of the Concordance of Vess and the prophesied Foretold One of the Warden Order, destined to bring balance to the Flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Kaelis earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Machine Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Nyxari, was created when Kaelis turned to the dark side of the Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce at the end of the Concordance Era.

A vergence in the Flux, Toren Kaelis was born on the desert planet of Kaldris in the Outer Rim Territories in 41 BBY. He was the son of Shmi Kaelis, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand midi-chlorians, surpassing Grand Master Thuun and all other Warden in the galaxy. Doran Vesk, the Warden Master who discovered Kaelis during the Invasion of Velmora in 32 BBY, theorized that Kaelis was conceived by the midi-chlorians. Following the Battle of Velmora, the Warden High Council admitted Toren Kaelis into the Order as the Acolyte of Warden Knight Maren Solveth despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Warden Code. Kaelis remained a Acolyte through the beginning of the Machine Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Archon Vale. Although Warden doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Toren Kaelis had a secret wife, Senator Ilyra Sundari of Velmora. During the early days of the Machine Wars, Kaelis served as a Warden General in the Vanguard Legion, commanding the conscript troopers of the elite 501st Legion against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Kaelis oversaw the Warden training of his own apprentice, Sera Vantis. By 19 BBY, when Tano walked away from the Warden Order, Kaelis began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Archon Vale during the Battle of Aurellon, Toren Kaelis's faith in the Warden was further shaken when he was put on the Warden Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce was Shael Vryce, the elusive Dark Lord of the Nyxari who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Warden had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Sundari in childbirth, Kaelis betrayed and turned against the Warden Order, believing Shael had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Warden Knight marched his legion on the Warden Temple of Aurellon where they executed its inhabitants, including Warden younglings, as part of Directive Zero and the Great Warden Purge. Velgor was then sent to Ashvane to exterminate the Sundered Council at the behest of his Nyxari Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Solveth, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Peren Kaelis and Princess Seren Corvath who were hidden from their father and the Emperor as the Dominion of Vess rose to power.

Being the Emperor's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Imperial fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Emperor's Nyxari apprentice throughout most of the Imperial Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Imperial Military. In the aftermath of killing Solveth in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Kesh, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Peren to the dark side. Peren sought to become a Warden, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the light side of the Flux. Velgor was defeated by Peren during the Battle of Sylvane in 4 ABY, but the young Warden refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Emperor to torture Peren with Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Toren, resulting in a redeemed Kaelis killing Shael at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Nyxari and fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One, Kaelis made peace with his son and became one with the Flux.

Toren Kaelis was one of the most powerful Warden and Nyxari in galactic history. Synth Flux was especially strong in the Kaelis bloodline, which culminated in Toren's grandson, Ewen Marsh, Corvath's son who was born by the end of the Galactic Civil War in 5 ABY. Marsh would turn to the dark side as well, becoming the dark warrior Kest Ren during the rise of the Ashfall Directorate. Like his grandfather, however, Marsh found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another-the Warden apprentice Nyra Solt, who heard Kaelis's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Nethyr in 35 ABY. Marsh's death ended the Kaelis bloodline, although Nyra Solt, the granddaughter of Shael, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

## Childhood on Kaldris

Believed to have been conceived by the midi-chlorians, Toren Kaelis was born to the slave Shmi Kaelis. Although Kaelis was listed as being born on the desert planet of Kaldris in some sources, confusion existed as to where Kaelis was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Kaldris, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Kaldris was Kaelis's homeworld. While living on Kaldris, Kaelis and his mother were originally owned by Gardulla the Vexkin until she lost them to the Toydarian Grix in a podracing bet when Kaelis was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Ani," he worked in his master's shop, located in Dunmar Reach.
