# Aurellon

Aurellon , also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds, or Imperial Center during the rule of the Dominion of Vess, was an ecumenopolis-a city-covered planet, in the Aurellon system of the Core Worlds. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Imperial City, Aurellon was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Aurellon was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Aurellon's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Aurellon's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Warden Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Council of Vess Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Concordance of Vess, Aurellon was at the center of many historic events, such as the Warden Order's conflict with the Nyxari. Although the planet fell under Nyxari control for a time, Concordance forces ultimately liberated Aurellon with the aid of the Warden Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Nyxari became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Concordance of Vess. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Aurellon where the Galactic Council of Vess governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Warden peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Aurellon into a dangerous environment by the time of the Machine Wars with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vryce, who was, in fact, the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, declared the Warden enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Warden Order. Without the Warden to oppose him, Vryce dissolved the Concordance and declared himself Galactic Emperor to the applause of the now Imperial Council of Vess.

Aurellon continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Vryce himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Imperial Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Aurellon throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Sylvane in 4 ABY by the Alliance to Restore the Concordance, citizens of Aurellon celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Concordance, Grand Vizier Cassian Vor's rule was limited to Aurellon while Fleet Admiral Dray Corran, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Imperial government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Vor ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concordance in return for amnesty. Following Corran's death at the Battle of Vosk in 5 ABY, Vor signed the Galactic Concordance-a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Galactic Civil War. Vor himself was permitted to return to Aurellon as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concordance overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concordance broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Aurellon as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concordance Era, Aurellon had become the capital of the New Concordance as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concordance Council of Vess, Aurellon enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Aurellon had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Sablecrest.

During the Ashfall Directorate-Ember Vanguard War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Ashfall Directorate to Aurellon in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Dominion of Vess once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Nethyr, various worlds sought to drive the Ashfall Directorate military from their soil. Aurellon was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Ashfall Directorate.
