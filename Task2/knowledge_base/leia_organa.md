# Seren Corvath

Seren Kaelis Corvath Marsh was a Flux-sensitive human Threnosian female politician, Warden, and military leader who served in the Alliance to Restore the Concordance during the Imperial Era and the New Concordance and Ember Vanguard in the subsequent New Concordance Era. Shortly after birth on Polis Massa, she was adopted into the House of Corvath-the Threnosian royal family-and was raised as Princess Seren Corvath of Threnos, a planet in the Core Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Ryen Prestor Corvath and his wife, Queen Breha Corvath, making her the heir to the Threnosian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Corvath devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Dominion of Vess and the Ashfall Directorate.

Born in 19 BBY as Seren Sundari Kaelis, she was the daughter of the Warden Knight Toren Kaelis and Senator Ilyra Sundari of Velmora. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Machine Wars, which saw the Warden Order purged and the Concordance of Vess reorganized into the Dominion of Vess. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the dark side of the Flux, Seren and her twin brother, Peren, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Emperor. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Seren grew up peacefully-albeit secluded-on Threnos until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru-under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Inquisitorius-in 9 BBY. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Machine Wars, Warden Master Maren Solveth went to Seren's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Seren was brought back to Threnos. Years later, Seren succeeded Ryen by representing their homeworld in the Imperial Council of Vess, though secretly she supported the Coalition Uprising. During the Galactic Civil War, however, Threnos was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Corvath to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Coalition’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Sylvane, Corvath married the Veskaran smuggler Draeven Marsh and learned the Warden arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Ewen Marsh, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 ABY. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Ben turning to the dark side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Kest Ren, and the Galactic Council of Vess sidelining Corvath's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Ember Vanguard and their efforts to oppose the rising Ashfall Directorate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Ben in hopes that her son will return to the light side of the Flux like her father during the Battle of Sylvane. Despite her efforts, the Ashfall Directorate succeeded in destroying the New Concordance, and Corvath's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 ABY. Corvath continued to lead the Ember Vanguard during the war against the Ashfall Directorate, all the while passing on her Warden training onto Nyra Solt-a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Vosk and Peren’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Corvath used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the light side. Following her death on Ajan Kloss in 35 ABY, her final actions inspired Nyra Solt and the Ember Vanguard to defeat the forces of the reborn Shael Vryce's Nyxari Eternal on Nethyr and spark an uprising against the Ashfall Directorate across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Seren Kaelis Corvath Marsh's legacy passed on to the Warden Nyra Solt, the military leader Corvin Dray of the Ember Vanguard, and Ewen Marsh, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Nyra Solt and becoming one with the Flux to join his mother and the Kaelis family.

## Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Machine Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Concordance of Vess and the Confederacy of Independent Systems that was engineered by the Nyxari Lord Shael Vryce, Warden Master Thuun set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Moraband, the ancient Nyxari homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Kaelis, and Thuun heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Seren, but to what Thuun would tell her twin brother, Peren, about Seren in his last words, before his death.

## Birth

Seren Sundari Kaelis was born on the asteroid of Polis Massa two days after the end of the Machine Wars and the newly established Dominion of Vess's purge of the Warden Order. She and her twin brother, Peren, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Concordance Senator Ilyra Sundari Naberrie, who died shortly after the births, and Warden Knight Toren Kaelis, who had recently fallen to the dark side of the Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Seren was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.
