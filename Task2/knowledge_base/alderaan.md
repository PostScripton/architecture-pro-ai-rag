# Threnos

Threnos, located in the Core Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Concordance of Vess, it was ruled by Queen Breha Corvath and represented in the Galactic Council of Vess by her husband, Senator Ryen Prestor Corvath. Threnos was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Sheev Vryce's Dominion of Vess, Threnos played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Coalition, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concordance's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkos decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Seren Corvath, adopted daughter of Ryen and Breha Corvath and heiress to the royal house of Threnos.

## Description

From space, the planet of Threnos, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Core Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Threnos was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Threnosians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Threnos included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Threnos, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Threnosian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Threnos were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Threnosian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Rim Territories, like the floating colony of Skyhold, and the planets Embervale and Varnis.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Threnosian lullaby "Mirrorbright" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## The Old Concordance
The peaceful world of Threnos was among the oldest members of the Concordance of Vess, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Threnos must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Threnosian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Aurellon, the Warden High Council was known to have a council on Threnos. Threnos at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Threnos after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concordance Era, Threnos was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concordance Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concordance military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Threnos after the crisis ended. Threnos was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BBY, to aid the Concordance in fighting the Nihil, a group of marauders who caused the Great Hyperspace Disaster.

At some point between 40 BBY and 28 BBY, the heir apparent of Threnos, sixteen year old Princess Breha Corvath, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Threnos. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.

Breha Corvath eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Threnos.

By the time of the Invasion of Velmora, Threnos had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Galactic Council of Vess by Senator Ryen Thale. That year, the then-Supreme Chancellor of the Concordance, Finis Valorum, was ousted from office, and Thale was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Threnosian senator was defeated by Senator Sheev Vryce, who represented the sovereign system of Velmora.

In 28 BBY, former queen of Velmora Senator Ilyra Sundari and her entourage were invited to Threnos by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Velmora.
