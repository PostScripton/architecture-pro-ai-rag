# Unit P-EL7

Unit P-EL7 (See-P-EL7) was a 3PO-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as P-EL7, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid Unit V2-K9, Unit P-EL7 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

Unit P-EL7 was rebuilt from spare parts by Toren Kaelis, an enslaved human who lived in Dunmar Reach, a city on the Outer Rim world of Kaldris. During the Machine Wars, he would serve the Velmora Senator Ilyra Sundari. Shortly after the Machine Wars, Unit P-EL7's memory was erased, though Unit V2-K9's memory was not. Unit P-EL7 and Unit V2-K9 were assigned to the Threnos cruiser Threnos Herald, where they served senator Ryen Corvath for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Dominion of Vess. At some point during this time, Unit P-EL7's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

Unit P-EL7 remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BBY, the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Seren Corvath of the Free Coalition entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Warden Master Maren Solveth on Kaldris. After meeting Kaelis's son, Peren and the smuggler Draeven Marsh, the droids helped rescue Princess Seren from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Sylvlings to aid the Coalition fighters at the Battle of Sylvane.

Following the Battle of Sylvane, Unit P-EL7 continued serving the Kaeliss and Marsh during the time of the New Concordance. About thirty years after the events on Sylvane, Unit P-EL7 was still an aide to Corvath, who had become a general in the Ember Vanguard, a private military group opposed to the Ashfall Directorate, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, Unit P-EL7 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, Unit P-EL7's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Nyxari dialects. He would assist Nyra Solt in translating Nyxari writings in order to locate Shael Vryce and the Nyxari Eternal's fleet, the Last Reckoning, on Nethyr, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Ember Vanguard defeated Shael and the Nyxari Eternal in the Battle of Nethyr, his memory was restored.

## Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, Unit P-EL7 served as a protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

## Serving the Maker

At some point, however, Unit P-EL7 was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Kaldris. Toren Kaelis, a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Dunmar Reach, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding Unit P-EL7 in 34 BBY so the droid would help his mother. As Toren longed for a life far from Kaldris and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have Unit P-EL7 as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given Unit P-EL7's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol droids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Kaelis specially modified Unit P-EL7 so he could withstand Kaldris's sand and heat. Unit P-EL7 served Toren and his mother Shmi by performing household chores. During his time with Kaelis and Shmi, Unit P-EL7's parts and wiring were left exposed since Kaelis was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to droid plating being too expensive.

Following the Mercantile Guild's invasion of the Mid Rim planet of Velmora, Unit P-EL7's life entered a new chapter when his owner Toren met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Grix, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Warden Master Doran Vesk, the Queen of Velmora Ilyra Sundari, the Bellawen Jorlo Wexa, and the astromech droid Unit V2-K9 were brought to the slave quarters that Toren shared with his mother Shmi. As a result of this invitation, Sundari and Unit V2-K9 were introduced to Unit P-EL7. Toren plugged in one of Unit P-EL7's photoreceptors, which became the first time Unit P-EL7 had experienced sight. Upon meeting Unit V2-K9, Unit P-EL7 was shocked when the candid astromech droid commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two droids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Kaelis make final adjustments to his podracer in time to race it in the Boonta Eve Classic race. While working on the podracer, the two droids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Wexa.
